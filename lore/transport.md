# Transport on Talan

*Consolidation file, 2026-09-25. Transport canon was spread across some forty files; this one gathers it in one place. It is the entry point and the summary: each section states the canon in brief and names the file that carries it in full. Where this file and a deeper file disagree, the deeper file wins for detail and this file wins for the continental frame (the rulings below). GM rulings of 2026-09-25 are marked* (GM, 2026-09-25).

**The frame.** Talan moves on four tiers, each with its own technology (full table in [`cosmology.md`](cosmology.md), *Transport: land, water, sky, Cloud Sea*):

| Tier | Craft | Technology | How common |
|---|---|---|---|
| Land | Magitrains; roads, rivers, caravans, beasts | Arcanotech (rail); none (everything else) | Rail common where it reaches; the road reaches everywhere else |
| Water | Saltkeels (Hafra), stillships (Midarra) | Wood-and-sail by count; Magitech the wealth upgrade; Occultech for chaos water | Everyday |
| Sky | Airships (incl. noonships) | Always Magitech: Arcanotech or Occultech | Rare over most of Talan, common over a few great cities |
| Cloud Sea | Cloudships | Always dual-school Magitech | Very rare; the only way off Talan |

The **Magitech** that matters for vessels: **Arcanotech** is formula-based, reproducible, industrial, and what "Magitech" means unqualified; chaos regions (the Basogur, the Blackened Lands, the Darklands) scramble its precision. **Occultech** runs on story and belief wound into the making; every piece is partly unique, it does not scale, and it holds up where Arcanotech fails. **Divitech** needs a god's continuing consent; **Primotech** is grown, and legendary. See [`cosmology.md`](cosmology.md), *The four sub-traditions of Magitech*.

## Land

### The rail

- **Magitrains** are Arcanotech and the public face of Magitech: scheduled passenger and freight service, common as roads where they reach. Most mortals of Talan have ridden one.
- **The Order of Steam invented them** at the end of the Dark Era (c. 2135 MR), out of its volcanic-belt Elden excavation; the Elden built rail-bound acceleration first, the Lost Era and the Golden Empire knew it possible, the Order built the working engine and the long lines. The rail is about four centuries old. The Order remains the principal maker of rolling stock for both networks.
- **The long lines follow older ground** (GM, 2026-09-25): in the north the Elden corridors, in the south the Golden Empire's roads.
- **Two networks, unlinked**, split by the Basogur Jungle:
  - **Northern**: confederated and multi-operator; the Order's licensed lines dominate, local lines exist. It has a **single commercial hub**, grown by trade-gravity at one of the flatter, cheaper rail cities; the city is not yet named. **Lurrath** is not it: the magirail runs a spur to a railhead at **Harrate**, Lurrath's gate, and the stone-oxen carry the last miles in ([`geography/brauogi.md`](geography/brauogi.md), *Lurrath*).
  - **Southern**: centred on **Merkavar**. Domains on it: Sumendar, Lautara, Lioaru, Egulon, Ezkudon, Zuzental, Nashavel.
- **The Order of Steam has no rail link** to the network it supplies (GM, 2026-09-25): its country is ringed by mountains and opens only east onto the Order of Steam bay, a Midarra sub-body with **Rika Tikur** in it. Whatever rail runs inside the ring stays inside it. Rolling stock ships out by the bay through Rika Tikur (and the Dreaming Cape), crosses by Lautara water and road, and enters the network at Merkavar ([`cosmology.md`](cosmology.md); [`geography/sumendar.md`](geography/sumendar.md); [`geography/lautara.md`](geography/lautara.md)). **Eldara**, outside the ring to the south, has its own single line to Merkavar through Tahu Tangata and Emarrea, one narrow valley deep ([`geography/sumendar.md`](geography/sumendar.md)).
- **Who runs the lines.** The Order licenses and sets freight terms (its autumn reduced-fare freight window on the southern network has held the same two weeks for forty years); **consortia** build and run lines (the Northern line in Villtur; the failed Basogur attempts); **chartered companies** run trunks (the **Everbright Line** in Harro Distiratsua, its charter due within the decade); landed houses hold stretches (Harro's **Lamphold houses**, "road and rail"); **rail capital** lobbies (Lua Lasai); local operators run short routes.
- **Who works them.** The **HRA**, the Halfling Railworkers' Association: the continental rail-labour union, branches at every major junction, headquarters at **Bellhalt** in Itsasalda, the largest voting bloc in Merkavar's assembly ([`geography/lautara.md`](geography/lautara.md); [`glossary.md`](glossary.md)).
- **Named lines and nodes:**

| Northern line | Touches Ehizahar at its settled edges; bends toward the railhead bid at Veidrath's edge, one clan's consent at a time | [`geography/ehizahar/villtur.md`](geography/ehizahar/villtur.md) |
| Magirail spur to Lurrath | Through Sugeiturri to the railhead at Harrate | [`geography/brauogi.md`](geography/brauogi.md) |

### Hazards on the rail

- **Train Pirates** hit the slow stretches (climbs, junctions, station approaches): *Langsam auf Land, schnell auf Schiene*. Most are banditry; some are political. The Order arms its licensed lines; smaller operators hire the Adventurers' Guild ([`cosmology.md`](cosmology.md); [`factions.md`](factions.md)).
- **The Conductor's Station**: a rare derailment into a starry pocket dimension, the Lost and Found of the rails; *"Tickets please!"* asks for a memory, an answer, or something of personal weight. It haunts both networks ([`cosmology.md`](cosmology.md)).
- **Single-track chokepoints**: the Arrol Cut, and Eldara's valley.

### Roads

- **Golden Empire roads** (560–1325 MR): paved and milestone-marked, the skeleton the southern rail later followed ([`timeline.md`](timeline.md); [`geography/_continent.md`](geography/_continent.md)).
- **Dark-Era roads** were deadly; **Uthra** coalesced from the prayers of those who walked them, and his worship is wayside: cairns, crossroad-stones, a coin at the fork ([`gods.md`](gods.md)). Harro's **Lamphold houses** came out of the dark owning the roads they kept lit, and still spend way-tolls on ways; Lua Lasai was the convoy-corridor between the fortress-ring and the **beacon-roads** ([`geography/egulon/harro-distiratsua.md`](geography/egulon/harro-distiratsua.md); [`geography/egulon/lua-lasai.md`](geography/egulon/lua-lasai.md)).
- **The long road** (GM, 2026-09-25: through the jungle; the old "bypass that loops around Basogur" is retired): eleven days from **Greenmouth** in Kaosadaemi to the **Oihandar Red Tusks' gate** by Veidrath, run on schedule by the **Roadwards**, freight on sledges, nights at the web-holds, Vanara guides; it carries what no airship can lift. Full canon: [`geography/nashavel/basogur.md`](geography/nashavel/basogur.md), [`geography/nashavel/kaosadaemi.md`](geography/nashavel/kaosadaemi.md), the crossing log [`geography/nashavel/basogur-log.md`](geography/nashavel/basogur-log.md).
- **Why overland on the northern side** (GM, 2026-09-25): port towns on Villtur's coasts are exceedingly rare, and most freeze in winter; **Fenurra's warm port** on the Hafra is the exception. Needs deepening (see *Still open*).
- **Other named roads.** Sugeiturri's *northern road*, "you'll never sleep cold", hot-spring towns for waystations ([`geography/brauogi/sugeiturri.md`](geography/brauogi/sugeiturri.md)); the **Crossroads**, three roads broadening into one sprawl of caravanserai ([`geography/zuzental.md`](geography/zuzental.md)); Harro's old refugee road, the route of the Lamp Ride; the Lua Lasai corridor road with its three condemned bridges; Lurrath's cut roads and graven ways, raised by the masons who build roads for half the continent; **Beatriz's road** in Argia Esfera, forbidden since 2524 ([`geography/egulon/argia-esfera.md`](geography/egulon/argia-esfera.md)).
- **Villtur** has no roads a clan did not agree to: crossing is by **the Handing** on **the strings** ([`geography/ehizahar/villtur.md`](geography/ehizahar/villtur.md)).

### Rivers, caravans, beasts

- **Rivers.** The Itsasalda river-trade is the working spine of the southern network's water side; stillships land at the Midarra coast and goods go on to Merkavar by river-craft or caravan-train; at Merkavar the lake is the street ([`geography/lautara.md`](geography/lautara.md)). River trade is held by Brauogi's old houses and Lioaru's River Duchies; Vernua's Maors own the quays, ferries, and roads over the Hegandi; Sombral's barges come down both rivers all night.
- **Caravans.** Most of Talan's overland trade passes through Lautara's caravan-and-train network ([`geography/lautara.md`](geography/lautara.md)). Izarelai's Open Eyes are bought by every caravan-master ([`geography/myrkono.md`](geography/myrkono.md)).
- **Beasts.** Stone-oxen, bred by the Haldmark Kholo for Lurrath's portage and the continent's heavy work; the Etxaber of the Villtur clans; the bond-beasts of the three hunting peoples; Fenurra's direwolves; the Fellibylur horse-breeders; Lioaru's Stokkul mounts; Valreka's sand-whales.

## Water

### The seas

- **Hafra**: the saltwater sea around Talan, ending at a sharp visible line against the Cloud Sea. **A Talan sea** (GM ruling in [`../docs/open-threads.md`](../docs/open-threads.md)): nothing lies on its far shore but the Cloud Sea.
- **Midarra**: the freshwater inner sea; Askamira and the Twin Cities sit in it; rarely violent, occasionally strange (**the Villveder**, stretches where magic turns unreliable) ([`geography/_continent.md`](geography/_continent.md)).
- **The Gazmuga**, the Line: the unmixing salt/sweet boundary at the Midarra mouth, touching **Balatur Erui** ([`geography/floteyn/balatur-erui.md`](geography/floteyn/balatur-erui.md)).
- **Sea access** by domain: the table in [`geography/_continent.md`](geography/_continent.md), *Domain sea-access summary*.

### Hulls

- **Saltkeel** (Hafra): brine-sealed, deep-keeled. **Stillship** (Midarra): lighter, shallower, untreated for brine. The split is a hull-class, independent of wood or Magitech; each sails the other sea badly. Cargo between the seas changes hulls at **Gesalkai** on Balatur Erui, across the Weighhouse scales ([`geography/_continent.md`](geography/_continent.md)).
- **Wood-and-sail** dominates by count; **Magitech ships** (mostly Arcanotech) are the wealth upgrade; **Occultech ships** work chaos water: near the Blackened Lands, Floteyn's deep eddies, the outer approaches to Sortalde. The Adventurers' Guild keeps a small Occultech sea fleet.
- The Order of Steam's **boiler-hulls** promise a sea that no longer needs forests, and Three Pines hears the promise ([`geography/myrkono.md`](geography/myrkono.md)).

### Pilots and charts

Each water is charted by the craft that reads it ([`geography/_continent.md`](geography/_continent.md), *Sea-charts*):
- **Open Hafra**: Fellibylur's **Stormriders**; master charts at **the Saltkeep**, Brasswatch, under the Skybell Republic, whose storm-bells ring the weather down the coast ([`geography/vindul.md`](geography/vindul.md)).
- **Midarra**: the Sweetwater League's **living chart** at the **List-house**, Uravel.
- **Bay-mouth and Midarra coast**: the Dreaming Cape's **Wakehelm**.
- **The gate**: Gesalkai's **Line-pilots**.
- **Instruments**: Breidey's **Helmworks** (bearing-keepers, drift-clocks, weatherclocks), precision Occultech only Breidey can make ([`geography/floteyn/breidey.md`](geography/floteyn/breidey.md)).
- Others who guide: Izarelai's **Starwrights** through the drift; Baerfrost's weather-readers as ice-route guides on the northern Hafra arc.

### Sea powers

- **Three Pines** (Myrkono): the premier Hafra shipwright; black **isiler** hulls launched at **Ontzola**; **the Unlit**, its dark escort fleet on the west Hafra ([`geography/myrkono.md`](geography/myrkono.md)).
- **The Sweetwater League** (Floteyn): League hulls run mail, passengers, and small freight to every point of the Midarra; **the Raft** its lashed-hull parliament; **Breidey** its great yard, whose keels broke the 2524 wave at sea ([`geography/floteyn/floating-isles.md`](geography/floteyn/floating-isles.md); [`geography/floteyn/breidey.md`](geography/floteyn/breidey.md)).
- **The Twin Cities**: the drifting pirate capital, a raft-city below and a city of tethered airships above; the Pirate Lords divide the seas and profit from the north-south crossing ([`geography/_continent.md`](geography/_continent.md)).
- **The Order of Steam and House Eisenhart**: the Leviathan-class warship, the Ironwing airships, and *Stahlglanz*, the amphibious fortress ([`geography/sumendar.md`](geography/sumendar.md)).
- **Uravel**: salvage and the wreck-trade; bell-craft that sink on purpose and come back ([`geography/floteyn.md`](geography/floteyn.md)).

### Crossings and ports

- **The standard Midarra crossing**: **Solkai** (Tvisol, Brauogi) ↔ **Rika Tikur** and the Dreaming Cape (Lautara), passing Frae City and the Twin Cities. This is bulk cargo's north-south route ([`cosmology.md`](cosmology.md); [`geography/brauogi/tvisol.md`](geography/brauogi/tvisol.md)).
- **Named ports** by water:
  - *Hafra*: Brasswatch, Cloudshadow, Highmarrow, Wrackmoor (the Skybell ports); Vindboorg; Grannholm; Ontzola and Geldur sound; Fiskhofn; Hverhofn on Urbero (ice-free); Fenurra's warm port (unnamed); Ferryhithe (the ferry to Zuzental's isles); Oathmoore (Emerald Isles).
  - *Midarra*: Rika Tikur; the Dreaming Cape; Bellhalt and the Muino-saila gap ports; Millhaven; Solkai and Hinstavor (Soul Tree); Breidhofn; Uravel; Gesalkai (both fronts); Baratalda's quay-town (unnamed); the Order of Steam bay.
  - *Lakes*: Merkavar; Ljosarn's hospice-quays; Sombral's river-lodges.
- **Ferries**: Solkai to the Soul Tree; Ferryhithe to Zuzental's Northern Isle; the Lua Lasai coast ferry to Jadrey; Vernua's morning boats to Nahaskel; Ljosarn's evening pilgrim ferries.

## Air

- **Every airship is Magitech.** **Arcanotech** airships lift on rune-arrays and run on engines, the Order of Steam's standard line. **Occultech** airships carry a **bound air elemental** as lift and co-pilot; slower in fair weather, better where chaos scrambles Arcanotech ([`cosmology.md`](cosmology.md)).
- **How common** (GM, 2026-09-25): rare over most of Talan, common over a few great cities, the god-cities above all. **Frae City**, the largest city on the continent, is the great airship port: berths along **the Rim** on the tower-tops, dear to rent ([`geography/askamira.md`](geography/askamira.md)).
- **How high** (GM, 2026-09-25): **low**. Airships fly well below the heights of the great ranges and route **around** mountain groups rather than over them; a sky-route follows passes and open water. The Order of Steam's airships leave its mountain ring through the bay and fly out over the Midarra. Rustam Varaz's flight from Merkavar to Sombral weaves through the mountains for the same reason and keeps off the southern way for the heat ([`geography/egulon/argia-esfera-letter.md`](geography/egulon/argia-esfera-letter.md)).
- **Noonships**: Occultech airships rated for the killing noon over Argia Esfera; every other ship crosses Argia in the morning or the dusk ([`geography/egulon/argia-esfera.md`](geography/egulon/argia-esfera.md)).
- **Over the Basogur**: the premium route for passengers and urgent freight. Arcanotech ships shake and drift in the chaos uplift; Occultech ships fly clean and charge for it. A millstone is too heavy for any airship that will take one ([`cosmology.md`](cosmology.md); [`geography/nashavel/basogur-log.md`](geography/nashavel/basogur-log.md)).
- **Who builds and flies them.** Vindul builds the best hulls of both kinds and supplies the elementals; the Order builds most Arcanotech engines; House Eisenhart builds the armoured Ironwings; Haizava flies cargo airships; the Strix are the natural pilots; airship crews keep Haizar ([`cosmology.md`](cosmology.md); [`geography/vindul.md`](geography/vindul.md); [`gods.md`](gods.md)). The Vindul and Sumendar airship guilds draw real revenue from the over-jungle route.
- **Masts and moorings**: Frae City (the Rim); Merkavar (several ports); Sombral (the mast on the highest roof, the reading chalked beside it); Lurrath (the gate-towers on the ring-crest; nothing fast crosses the threshold); Veidrath (the docking-tower district); Lograth; the Twin Cities' tethered airships.
- **Within cities**: Haizava's svifs and windrifts on the wind canals; Lograth's **Arteries**, glyph-routed aerial lines with gondolas; Frae City's chain-stairs and lifts.
- **Flying mounts** exist (GM, 2026-09-25), and they are rare, regional, and very elite: each kind belongs to the place and people that breed or bond it, and a king or a prince may own one where a common noble does not. Elsewhere, one overhead is a marvel. The kinds are defined region by region, at each build.

## The Cloud Sea and beyond

- **The Cloud Sea** bears no weight: any ordinary hull or airship that goes onto it sinks and vanishes ([`cosmology.md`](cosmology.md), *The Cloud Sea*).
- **Cloudships** are always dual-school: Arcanotech with Occultech as standard (the Occultech lets the ship "remember the way", its routes kept as song); a few with Divitech (the Adventurers' Guild's one or two, the churches of Iro and Fisaya); *Eyrasunda*, the living-tree cloudship, legendary. Very rare; Vindul builds the best hulls ([`cosmology.md`](cosmology.md)).
- **A cloudship rides the vapour** (GM, 2026-09-25). Its dual-school binding makes the Cloud Sea bear it the way water bears a boat: it sits on the white, sails it, and anchors in it. Nothing flies over the Cloud Sea: an airship's lift fails above it and the ship goes down into the vapour with the rest. The same hull sails water, which is why the very wealthy keep cloudships as luxury vessels. A **cloud-quay** is a berth where the Cloud Sea meets rock, and only the two islands that touch the Cloud Sea have such a shore; every cloud-quay on Talan stands in the Bridgelands, and a quay on Jadrey would make it the second landing.
- **Only two places on Talan touch the Cloud Sea** (GM, 2026-09-25), and both are islands: the **Bridgelands** of the Emerald Isles (Zuzental), the canonical cloudship landing, where the Arcanotech bridges carry caravans across the gaps ([`geography/zuzental.md`](geography/zuzental.md)); and **Jadrey** off Lua Lasai (Egulon), which keeps no cloud-quay and no mast by island law ([`geography/egulon/lua-lasai.md`](geography/egulon/lua-lasai.md)).
- **Off Talan**: **Sortalde**, east across the Cloud Sea, builds no cloudships; every ship on the route is Talan-made, and it reaches only the outer petals. **The Red Empire's home continent**, west across the Cloud Sea (GM, 2026-09-25): the **Iron Tide**'s warship-cloudships cross it and come down onto Hafra as a navy ([`geography/_off-continent.md`](geography/_off-continent.md)).
- **The Riseway**: the sky-lane over Balatur Erui that every chart marks and no keel uses, the skywhales' climb ([`geography/floteyn/balatur-erui.md`](geography/floteyn/balatur-erui.md)).

## Getting north and south

The Basogur splits the rail, and the continent crosses its waist four ways:

| Way | Route | Character |
|---|---|---|
| Stillship across the Midarra | Rika Tikur / Dreaming Cape ↔ Solkai, by Frae City and the Twin Cities | Bulk cargo's route; the Pirate Lords take their share |
| Airship over the Basogur | Over the canopy | Premium passengers and urgent freight; Occultech flies it clean |
| The long road | Greenmouth → the Red Tusks' gate by Veidrath, eleven days | What no airship can lift; the Roadwards keep it on schedule |
| By the northern coast | Villtur's shore | Barely an option: rare ports, most frozen in winter; Fenurra's warm port the exception |

**Known travel times**: the long road, eleven to twelve days; Lua Lasai by the Thekkavar express, half a day; a weatherclock reads no further than a day's sail.
