# Egulon: Light · Iro

**Borders:** Ezkudon, Lautara, Zuzental

**Seas:** Hafra (southern coast; large forested island off the south-east). **No Midarra access.**

**Etymology:** Basque *eguzkia* (sun) → compressed and drifted to *Egulon*. Names the warmth and golden quality of the domain: sunlight on open fields, not cold brilliance.

**Position:** Southeast Talan, east of Ezkudon, south of Zuzental and Nashavel, coastal on Hafra

**Terrain:** Warm golden terrain throughout, open and relatively flat. Mountain ranges scattered through center and west. Distinctive circular mountain ring with forest to the east. Large forested island off the southeast coast. The most settled and prosperous domain on the continent. Egulon's warm south-facing slopes are now Talan's wine country: the vineyard-and-cellar trade that once sat oddly in cool Azkataria (Lautara) was rehomed here to the Light domain's sunlight, where wine belongs (full Egulon wine-country canon pending; see [`../../docs/open-threads.md`](../../docs/open-threads.md), *[Azkataria] Open Floor rebuild*).

**God's city:** Ljosarn, The Everbright City (stands in Harro Distiratsue, on the lake bordering Argia Esfera, where all three of Egulon's peoples meet; detailed in its own pass)

**Sub-regions:**
- **Harro Distiratsue**: fully within Egulon, no longer a border territory. *(Basque harro "proud" + distiratsu "radiant": "Proud Radiance.")* The Lautara/Egulon border shifted west to absorb the sub-region entirely; the proud-radiance reading sits naturally inside the Light domain. **Sprite-dominant; the culture is the light-craft.** Harro Distiratsue measures standing by the brightness it can make: the lantern-work and living-light of a harvest night, the illumination that lifts a press-night into festival, the staged brilliance of a high rite on the lake. Made-light is held holy here, and the craft belongs to the place rather than to any one people: a Leshy of Harro grows lantern-vines for the light-courts, a Shoony of Harro keeps a festival-shrine, and both work in the same proud register as their Sprite neighbours. Ljosarn stands on the sub-region's lake, on the Argia Esfera border.
- **Lua Lasai**: border territory shared with Ezkudon; the calm grove-and-vine farm-march. **Leshy-dominant; the culture is husbandry-courtesy.** Land in Lua Lasai is tended in consultation with the lineages that remember it: a planting is not finished when the stock is in the ground, but when the grove's Leshy has taken to the young vine and given it a name, and a farmer who skips the courtesy is held to have planted in haste. The custom is Lua Lasai's own, and every people farming here keeps it. See also [Ezkudon](ezkudon.md).
- **Argia Esfera**: *"sphere of light."* Basque *argia* (light) + Latin/Spanish *esfera* (sphere). An old compound name fitting the Light domain's character. **Shoony-dominant; the culture is the kept flame.** Argia Esfera is the devout country of the domain, named for the great flame at its heart that has not gone dark in living memory, tended in unbroken shifts as the sub-region's central institution. Almost every Shoony heart here is given to Iro, and the devotion wears three faces: the warm welcome of the open door, the bright zeal whose certainty powers the church's outward energy, and the healer's hope that holds the dark off. The faith runs hottest in Argia Esfera, the vigil is the honoured work, and made-light is watched with the most suspicion: a Sprite in Argia Esfera learns to dim, or to dazzle very carefully.

## The Year of the Vine

Egulon is Talan's wine country, and the wine is the truest thing about it. Where other domains read the Light god as doctrine or as the sun overhead, Egulon reads him as the warmth that ripens a grape, and the work of the domain is the slow turning of sunlight into something a mortal can hold, pour, and offer up. The year of the vine is the calendar everything here keeps: the budding, the long ripening through the killing heat, the harvest, the press, and the cellar-dark where last year's light waits out the winter. Three peoples share that year, each leaning into it by temperament without being bound to it, and the shape of Egulon is the shape of how they hold together.

Three peoples share that year, and the domain-level read is a feeling of each people, never a practice: the customs live in the sub-regions above, and a Leshy of Harro Distiratsue, a Sprite of Argia Esfera, and a Shoony of Lua Lasai each live the culture of where they are, in the temperament of what they are.

**The Leshy are the ones who stay.** Plant-spirits and a full people, born and raising children and holding lineages like any other folk, the Leshy carry rootedness as temperament: to stay, to tend, to remember. The plant-spirits of wetter lands wander and self-seed; the Egulon Leshy keep to their ground across generations, and in a domain where mortal families turn over every few lifetimes they are the part that remembers, who planted this windbreak, what stood in this field before the vines, what the soil took badly the last time someone tried it. The feeling travels with them wherever in the domain they live; the work it turns to is the place's own.

**The Sprites are the ones who shine.** Settled and Material-born, a world away in temperament from their flighty Feyworld cousins, the Egulon Sprites feel outward: delight, grief, mischief, and pride all run straight to the surface and come out as light, glamour worn openly and well. A Sprite who is happy is a room that knows it, and where a Sprite lives there is more light than the sun strictly provides. The fey-and-chaos lineage is still in them, and whether their brilliance honours Iro or only shows off in his colours is a question Egulon has never settled.

**The Shoony give the heart once.** Loyalty and constancy as one temperament: a Shoony who has chosen you has chosen for good, and the choosing does not wear out. Many as the breeds, a dozen dog-kinds under one name, and underneath every coat the same fact: a Shoony is the one still there, at the door, at the altar, at the sickbed, when everyone else has gone home.

**The two lights, and the weight beneath them.** Egulon's life runs along the seam between its two bright peoples. The Shoony keep the true light, the constant flame that must not die; the Sprites throw the made light, the dazzling spark of the festival; and the two do not wholly trust each other, the one suspecting display where there should be reverence, the other finding the vigil joyless. That argument is the domain's weather. Beneath it sits the rootedness of the Leshy, who decline the quarrel, because a contest of festival-light against flame-light is the business of a single short lifetime and a Leshy lineage has watched the vines through ten of them. On anything that touches the soil both bright peoples defer to the rooted one; the courtesies that deference takes are each region's own. The hot two argue; the cool one holds the weight. And at the lake where Harro Distiratsue meets Argia Esfera, on the very line of the seam, stands Ljosarn, the Everbright City, where all three peoples meet, which is a story for its own telling.
