# Egulon: Light · Iro

**Borders:** Ezkudon, Lautara, Zuzental

**Seas:** Hafra (southern coast; large forested island off the south-east). **No Midarra access.**

**Etymology:** Basque *eguzkia* (sun) → compressed and drifted to *Egulon*. Names the warmth and golden quality of the domain: sunlight on open fields, not cold brilliance.

**Position:** Southeast Talan, east of Ezkudon, south of Zuzental and Nashavel, coastal on Hafra

**Terrain:** Warm golden terrain throughout, open and relatively flat. Mountain ranges scattered through center and west. Distinctive circular mountain ring with forest to the east. Large forested island off the southeast coast. The most settled and prosperous domain on the continent. Egulon's warm south-facing slopes are now Talan's wine country: the vineyard-and-cellar trade that once sat oddly in cool Azkataria (Lautara) was rehomed here to the Light domain's sunlight, where wine belongs (full Egulon wine-country canon pending; see [`../../docs/open-threads.md`](../../docs/open-threads.md), *[Azkataria] Open Floor rebuild*).

**God's city:** Ljosarn, The Everbright City (stands in Harro Distiratsue, on the lake bordering Argia Esfera, where all three of Egulon's peoples meet; detailed in its own pass)

**Sub-regions:**
- **Harro Distiratsue**: fully within Egulon, no longer a border territory. *(Basque harro "proud" + distiratsu "radiant": "Proud Radiance.")* The Lautara/Egulon border shifted west to absorb the sub-region entirely; the proud-radiance reading sits naturally inside the Light domain. **Heartland of the Sprites**, and home to Ljosarn on its lake.
- **Lua Lasai**: border territory shared with Ezkudon; calm, settled farm-march. **Heartland of the Leshy**, the rooted keepers of Egulon's long-cultivated ground. See also [Ezkudon](ezkudon.md).
- **Argia Esfera**: *"sphere of light."* Basque *argia* (light) + Latin/Spanish *esfera* (sphere). An old compound name fitting the Light domain's character. **Heartland of the Shoony** and the devotional core of the domain, named for the great kept flame at its heart.

## The Year of the Vine

Egulon is Talan's wine country, and the wine is the truest thing about it. Where other domains read the Light god as doctrine or as the sun overhead, Egulon reads him as the warmth that ripens a grape, and the work of the domain is the slow turning of sunlight into something a mortal can hold, pour, and offer up. The year of the vine is the calendar everything here keeps: the budding, the long ripening through the killing heat, the harvest, the press, and the cellar-dark where last year's light waits out the winter. Three peoples share that year, each leaning into it by temperament without being bound to it, and the shape of Egulon is the shape of how they hold together.

**The Leshy keep the ground.** In Lua Lasai, the calm settled march where Egulon runs up against the Knowledge domain, the Leshy are a rooted people. The plant-spirits of wetter lands wander and self-seed; the Egulon Leshy stay, born and raising children and holding lineages like any other folk, an old line keeping to the same grove, orchard-row, and vine-stock across generations. In a domain where mortal families turn over every few lifetimes, the Leshy are the part that remembers: who planted this windbreak, what stood in this field before the vines, what the soil took badly the last time a farmer tried it here. A new planting in Egulon is not finished when the stock is in the ground; it is finished when the grove's Leshy has taken to the young vine and given it a name, and a farmer who skips the courtesy is held to have planted in haste. The Leshy keep the ground, and the ground keeps the year.

**The Sprites make the light.** Harro Distiratsue, the Proud Radiance, is the Sprites' country, and the Egulon Sprites are settled and Material-born, a world away in temperament from their flighty Feyworld cousins. Over generations under Iro's sun they turned fey-glamour into a craft they are openly proud of: the radiance Egulon is famous for. The lantern-work and living-light of a harvest night, the illumination that lifts a press-night into a festival, the staged brilliance of a high rite at Ljosarn, all of it is Sprite-made, and a court measures itself by the brightness it can throw. In Harro Distiratsue that pride is celebrated and the made-light is held holy. Carry the same Sprites into the devout country, though, and the welcome cools: where the faith runs hottest they are watched as fey-and-chaos wearing the colours of the Light, and whether their brilliance honours Iro or only shows off in his name is a question Egulon has never settled.

**The Shoony keep the faith.** The Shoony are the light of Iro carried across the whole domain, loyal as the dogs they take after and many as the breeds: a dozen dog-kinds under one name, almost all of them of the faith. Their devotion wears three faces. There is the warm welcome, the Shoony at the open door and the roadside table who make Egulon the most hospitable country on the continent. There is the bright zeal, the fervent faithful whose certainty powers the church's outward energy. And there are the healers of Hope, who tend the sick and the despairing and carry Iro's light as the thing that holds the dark off. Their heartland is Argia Esfera, the Sphere of Light, and at its centre they keep a great flame that has not gone dark in living memory, tended in unbroken shifts so that the light of the domain is always, in the plainest sense, kept. To a Shoony, loyalty and constancy are one word: the flame stays lit because a Shoony is always there.

**The two lights, and the weight beneath them.** Egulon's life runs along the seam between its two bright peoples. The Shoony keep the true light, the constant flame that must not die; the Sprites throw the made light, the dazzling spark of the festival; and the two do not wholly trust each other, the one suspecting display where there should be reverence, the other finding the vigil joyless. That argument is the domain's weather. Beneath it sits the Leshy, who decline the quarrel, because a contest of festival-light against flame-light is the business of a single short lifetime and a Leshy has watched the vines through ten of them. They keep the ground both sides stand on, and on anything that touches the soil both sides defer to them: a Sprite cannot claim a grove for a festival the local Leshy has not blessed, and a Shoony cannot consecrate a first-pressing the Leshy did not see ripen. The hot two argue; the cool one holds the weight. And at the lake where Harro Distiratsue meets Argia Esfera, on the very line of the seam, stands Ljosarn, the Everbright City, where all three peoples meet, which is a story for its own telling.
