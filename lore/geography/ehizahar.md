# Ehizahar: Hunt · Hinka

**Borders:** Nashavel, Vindul, Brauogi

**Seas:** Hafra (northern arctic coast; Ardo Beroa ice-isle archipelago offshore).

**Etymology:** Basque *ehiza* (hunt) + *zahar* (ancient, old) → *ehiza-zahar* → naturally eroded to *Ehizahar*. The ancient hunt. The oldest name on Talan, for the oldest land.

**Position:** Northeast Talan: the largest domain, spanning arctic north to jungle south

**Terrain:** The most varied domain. Arctic/icy tundra in the far north, with a small ice-isle archipelago off the northern coast. The highest mountain range on the continent and the oldest trees (ancient dark conifers, northwest corner). Open grassland through the center. Deep jungle-like old-growth forest in the south and southeast. Rivers throughout. The domain that feels most primordially "of the world itself." A meteor struck deep antiquity, leaving a vast crater that still bleeds slow volcanism into the surrounding land: the **Fenurra** sub-region, the only significant volcanic activity outside Sumendar.

**God's city:** Veidrath, The Hunting City

**Peoples:** Three dominant ancestries are anchored here: **Orc**, **Lizardfolk**, and **Goloma**, the three phases of one hunt; beside them stand the **Fenurran tribes** of the crater-country, several peoples united by one wyrm-blood (see *Fenurra* below). The domain-level read is a feeling of each people, never a practice; the cultures live with the sub-regions, and Hinka's faces sit behind the three as inspiration: Predation, Patience, Survival. Her Cruelty is the face nature itself claims.

- **The Orcs close.** Predation as temperament: the world met head-on, now, holding back the one unbearable thing. Loudest, first in, most consistently in feud; Hinka is depicted as a red-headed orc, and the affinity runs deep. The hunt ends with them.
- **The Lizardfolk never stop coming.** Patience as temperament: slow to start and impossible to shake. A Lizardfolk decision takes its time arriving, and once made it is inevitable; the people of the long stalk, deliberate at the kill, unhurried in a way that frightens prey more than speed would.
- **The Goloma see it first.** Survival as temperament: the hunt known from the prey's side. Many-eyed, secretive, patient at the quiet edges, a Goloma is the one who noticed before anyone else and said nothing yet. A people shaped by being hunted, at home among the hunters, because nobody reads a predator better than the watched.

The Goloma see it, the Lizardfolk run it down, the Orcs bring it low: one hunt in three peoples, and the Villtur feuds are what happens when the three phases of a hunt share one land and each believes its phase is the essential one. **Cruelty** is the face nature itself claims: not the cruelty of machinations but the cruelty of survival, the lean winter, the kill that feeds, some dying so that others may live. No hunter needs to be cruel for the hunt to have cruelty in it; the land holds that face, and the tribes leave it there.

**The roaming and the rooted.** The present age runs one tension through every clan of all three peoples: most still ride the hunt-circuits as they always have, but a growing number have begun to settle, villages at the river-fords, towns where the rail touches the domain's edges. The question every settling clan answers differently is whether the hunt survives an address: a settled Goloma watches a town the way her grandmother watched a trail, a settled Orc finds the crush of market-day carries something of a well-led charge, and the roaming clans have not decided whether their settled cousins are scouts in new country or prey that built its own pen.

**Sub-regions:**
- **Lands of Villtur**: the umbrella tribal territory covering most of Ehizahar. The domain is overwhelmingly tribal: no unified kingdoms, no formal borders. The many tribes that live across the arctic tundra, grasslands, and ancient forests all fall under this collective name. Veidrath sits within this expanse. *(Icelandic villtur = wild, lost, strayed; old name, fits the ancient naming rule.)*
- **Fenurra**: a sub-region of Ehizahar in its own right, bordering the Lands of Villtur but distinct from them. Defined by an ancient meteor crater (the **Scar of Aeris**, capital city **Aeris** carved into its inner walls) and the volcanic activity that still surrounds it: sulfurous fumes, basalt fields, slow lava flows, and strange star-metals seeded into the ground by the impact. Harsh and resource-poor in conventional terms, but rich in materials no other place on Talan produces (Blackglass, Velthite, Ghost Willow, Obsidian Silk). The Fenurran tribes who live here are hardened, pragmatic, and politically distinct from the wider Villtur tribal sprawl. The tribes are united by the **Dragonblood** heritage rather than by ancestry: each tribe its own major people, one wyrm-blood through all four (per-tribe roster canon-pending; the full Fenurra rework is its own pass). The only significant volcanic activity outside Sumendar. **Full canon: [`ehizahar/fenurra.md`](ehizahar/fenurra.md)** (four tribes Draconis · Vexiren · Brakkaun · Seravain, Speaker's Mantle politics, Bone Gong + War Council, materials, warfare doctrine, Notable Weapons table, the schism with the Divine Faith).
- **Basogur Jungle**: the deep jungle territory in the south and southeast, shared with Nashavel. See also [Nashavel](nashavel.md). *(Continental rail-blocker; see the Nashavel entry above for the full breakdown.)*
- **Ardo Beroa**: the small ice-isle archipelago off Ehizahar's northern arctic coast. *(Basque ardo = wine + beroa = hot, warm → "warm wine" or "hot wine." Old name, etymology partially obscured by drift; the ironic mismatch between the literal meaning and the arctic character is part of the local lore.)*
