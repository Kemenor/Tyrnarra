*Voices inside the moment (passing):* **[LEADER]**, the party's leader, terse and first into the fight; **[SHIELD]**, who counts aloud under pressure and asks the plain question; **[CASTER]**, who says little; **the Skeleton** of the cursed land, plain-spoken, in Talanese with an old shape to it; **the woman**, living, gentle and absolute.

---

**Whalehall · the vault under Anzar · relic ledger**

**Item:** a lantern of bronze, Storveldi make, its glass intact; cold to the touch whatever the room.
**Recovered by:** a Roll party of four, Platinum, contract out of Tassast's watch-works. Three returned: **[HOLDER]**, **[CASTER]**, **[SHIELD]**. The party's leader, **[LEADER]**, did not; body not recovered.
**Commission:** a recovery for a collector on the Brokers' Walk. The client handled the item once and **declined delivery**. Held in the vault at the party's request; not for sale.
**Property:** the lantern holds a moment. Whoever takes it by the handle sees and hears what its carrier saw and heard on the day, start to finish, about the length of a long breath, and then it begins again if the hand stays. The moment is the same every time. Holders report that the last sound stays in the ears for a day or more.
**Handling:** by the ring only. Do not take the handle.
**Transcribed by:** [APPRAISER] Anzar, appraiser, the Brokers' Walk, 2531 MR.

*The transcription follows as the moment plays: what is seen, what is heard, what is said. Nothing else carries.*

---

Haze. Black sand in the air, fine as flour, and the sun up there somewhere, a copper coin behind a cloth. The lantern swings in the carrier's hand and lights ten paces of it.

A street. Walls of dressed stone, clean-edged, as if they were cut last year. Then the walls go on and there are bones in the sand along them, a great many, and then the bones are standing up.

"Back to back." That is [LEADER]. "Back to back and walk."

They come out of the sand in their dozens with no sound but the sand running off them. Steel on bone. [SHIELD] counting them aloud and losing count. [CASTER] saying a word and the haze lighting white, and the street full of falling bones. The lantern swings. Twenty paces. Thirty. The party walks backward through a square with a dry fountain in it, and the dead keep getting up.

Then they stop getting up. All of them at once. They stand where they are and turn their skulls the same way, up the street, and wait.

Something comes down the street in armour. Rust from the helm to the feet, and taller than anyone the carrier has stood beside, and under the rust the armour is very old. It walks without hurrying. The standing dead step aside for it.

"Behind me," [LEADER] says, and goes to meet it.

It is quick, after that. One blow turned. One blow that is not. [LEADER] goes down on the stones of the square and does not move, and the rusted thing lifts its blade again, and the carrier is running at it, and the lantern is swinging wild.

A voice in the haze, speaking words the living do not know: old words, slow, with a weight on every one.

The rust on the armour catches fire. Blue fire, from the inside, and the thing turns toward the voice. Out of the haze comes a Skeleton in a long dark robe, a staff in both hands, and behind it two more: a big shape, wrong in its limbs, a Fleshwarp, who puts both hands into the black sand and drags it up into a wall; and a woman, living, plain-faced, who walks into the square, and every standing bone in it lies down. They lie down like dogs. They do not get up again.

The rusted thing burns. It fights in the fire for a long time, and the Skeleton's voice never stops, and then the armour is empty and falls apart on the stones.

Quiet. Sand settling.

[SHIELD], breathing hard: "Who are you?"

The Skeleton, in Talanese with an old shape to it: "We live here."

[CASTER], kneeling by [LEADER]. Then not kneeling, standing up. Shaking the head.

The woman, gently: "You cannot leave him in this ground."

[SHIELD]: "We'll carry him."

The woman: "You will not carry him past the ridge before he gets up. He will march with them by morning."

"With who?"

Nobody answers. The Fleshwarp has lifted its head and is listening, and the Skeleton has turned its skull the way the dead turned theirs, up the street.

It is not a drum. It is feet. A great many, in step, slow, iron on bone and bone on stone, from somewhere inside the haze, and it is not getting louder fast. It does not have to.

The Skeleton: "Go. Now. We will see to him."

The lantern swings toward the haze, and the sound goes on.
