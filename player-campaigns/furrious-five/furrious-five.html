Where You Stand

You are based in Millhaven, a town of some six thousand straddling the mouth of the Smáa where the border river parts the dreaming Cape from steadfast Itsasalda. Two banks, three bridges, and no one wholly in charge of the water between them. It is a working crossing, a market grown by accident, and exactly the kind of place where unlisted cargo, an honest fight, and a quiet word all change hands within a hundred paces of each other.

Your work comes from the Wayward Compass, Millhaven's Adventurers' Guild Office, on the widest of the three bridges under a faded gold compass-rose. You take what hangs on its board, you drink where the guild actually drinks, and you learn, soon enough, that the southernmost bridge has a fighting pit under it and that the pit has floors the deed does not mention. This companion holds the places you'll come to know. The world they sit in is one click further out, on the worldbuilding pages each links to.

Reading These Pages

Everything here is yours to read. The Quest Board shows you every posting the way it hangs on the wall: the notice, the pay, who to ask, and what is plainly known. What waits at the end of a contract is not written down here, and finding it out is the point. The location pages tell you what your characters would know after a few weeks in town, no more and no less.
