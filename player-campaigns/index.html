What This Is

The rest of this site is the world: the gods, the domains, the history, written as a chronicler of Talan would write it, true whether or not anyone is adventuring through it. These pages are something narrower and closer to the table. They are the player's companion to a campaign actually being run: the town the party calls home, the places they walk into, the people behind the bar, and the contracts pinned to the guild board.

Everything here is written for players and meant to be read freely. It carries no answers the party hasn't earned yet: what a posting on the board says, you may read; what is actually waiting at the end of it, you find out the way everyone does. Each campaign has its own look. Open one to step inside it.
