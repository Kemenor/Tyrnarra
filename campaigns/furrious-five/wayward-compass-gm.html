The Building

The Wayward Compass has occupied its spot on the central bridge since 1,891 MR, over six hundred years, through the tail end of the Dark Era and everything after. It did not start as a guildhall. It started as a storage shed that a group of adventurers rented by the week, then could not stop renting because it was convenient, and then quietly bought when the owner died without heirs.

It has grown the way the bridge district has grown: upward first, then outward, then eventually downward onto pilings that were originally just meant to support a practice post for Kael's sparring. The original shed is now somewhere inside the walls of the second floor. Nobody is entirely sure which walls. Rumour has it the shed still houses an artifact of the original trio who founded the hall. Aldric will scowl if you go looking for it.

From the bridge level it presents a respectable face: a wide door, the compass-rose sign, Mira Seld's desk visible through the front window. From the river it looks like something that decided it wanted to be a boat and could not commit, ropes running between the piling extensions and the bridge above. The sound of the river is constant, louder in the lower levels, a soft undertone in the upper floors. Regulars stop noticing it. New arrivals find it either calming or unsettling.

What Makes It Feel Like Home
Maps cover almost every wall: some current, some historical, some of places nobody has been able to identify for decades. Trophy cases line the drinking-hall stairs: a basilisk scale, a piece of what might be Elden stonework, three weapons of unusual make with small plaques listing the names of the people who brought them back. The plaques list when they were recovered. They do not always list what happened to the person who recovered them. Regulars know which ones to avoid reading.

Mira Seld
Branch Receptionist · half-elf, mid-30s
Tap ▾
The first face every adventurer sees, and the last one they want to disappoint. Mira runs the front desk with terrifying competence and a smile that never quite reaches her eyes.
Personality
Unflappable. Warm but watchful. She remembers every name, every face, every outstanding fee. Speaks to hardened veterans the same way she speaks to terrified first-timers: calmly, clearly, with exactly the right amount of expectation.
Appearance
Practical dark hair pinned back, ink-stained fingers, three quills always tucked behind her ear. Wears the guild uniform, navy and gold, with the precision of someone who ironed it that morning and will iron it again tomorrow.
Notable Traits
  • Knows every active contract, who took it, and when it is overdue
  • Can de-escalate a barfight using only tone of voice
  • Sends handwritten follow-up notes after dangerous missions
  • Fiercely protective of new recruits; quietly tests veterans
Guild Stats
Perception +8Insight +9Persuasion +7Rank 7 (Retired Field)
⚿ GM Secret
Mira is a former field agent, Rank 7 clearance, who burned out after a classified operation went wrong. The Head Office offered a desk as a mercy posting. She took it, and discovered she is better at this than she ever was at fieldwork. She could go back. She will not.
⚓ Hooks
  • She quietly steers particular quests to particular parties. Why?
  • An old contact shows up at the desk pale and panicked; Mira sends everyone out of earshot.
  • A sealed letter arrives addressed to her with a Head Office sigil she recognises and visibly pales at.

Aldric Fenn
Branch Guildmaster · Bridge Council Seat · human, late 50s
Tap ▾
A bear of a man who runs a small-town office for the most powerful organisation in the known world, and is acutely aware of both halves of that sentence. Drinks too much. Cares too much. Gets results.
Personality
Direct, pragmatic, occasionally volcanic. Prefers solving problems with a handshake, but keeps a shortsword on the desk for symbolic reasons. Treats his branch like family, which means he worries constantly and never says so.
Appearance
Big, weathered, with the kind of scarring that tells stories. Salt-and-pepper beard, always slightly unkempt. Wears his guildmaster's medallion but tucks it under his collar when meeting locals; he does not want them afraid of what it represents.
Notable Traits
  • Has a file on every guild branch within 200 miles, some of it unofficial
  • Will bend rules for his people, will not break the Charter, knows the difference intimately
  • Refers to Head Office as "the suits" with deep personal feeling
  • Never turns away someone in genuine need, even if they cannot pay the registration fee
Guild Stats
Athletics +7Intimidation +8History +6Insight +7Veteran Field Agent
⚿ GM Secret
Aldric was offered a Regional Director position three times. He turned it down each time. Head Office sent an auditor to find out why. Aldric bought them a beer, explained nothing, and the auditor left confused but oddly at peace. The real reason: he knows what Regional Directors are actually asked to do, and some of it keeps him up at night.
⚓ Hooks
  • He pulls a party aside after a job and says "this one doesn't go in the official report."
  • A Head Office inspector arrives unannounced with a perfect smile and cold eyes.
  • He asks the party to quietly look into a guild branch two towns over that has gone silent.

Sister Velka Dorn
Quartermaster & Archivist · human (lapsed cleric), 40s
Tap ▾
Manages the equipment locker, the contract archive, and the deep records room, the last of which most people do not know exists. Was once a cleric of a knowledge deity; now serves a different kind of truth.
Personality
Meticulous, precise, with flashes of dark humour that appear without warning. Has opinions about how things are stored, filed, and returned. Will lend equipment to a party she trusts; will not lend her good ink to anyone.
Appearance
Tall, angular, hair gone silver early. Reading glasses she refuses to admit she needs. Carries a ring of keys too large for the building's known doors. Wears simple grey robes with a guild badge pinned exactly level.
Notable Traits
  • Knows where every piece of guild equipment is, including the pieces that are not logged
  • Has read every contract filed at this branch going back 30 years
  • Can authenticate a document in seconds and spot a forgery in less
  • Does not gossip. Does tell you what you need to know, sometimes unprompted.
Guild Stats
History +10Investigation +9Religion +7Arcana +6Former Rank 5 (Church Transfer)
⚿ GM Secret
The deep records room holds files on guild operations that predate the current Charter. Velka has read them all. Some describe things that, technically, the guild should not have survived doing. She catalogued them, said nothing, and started keeping her own coded notes. She serves the guild. She also serves what the guild claims to stand for. She is watching to see if those things stay the same.
⚓ Hooks
  • She pulls an old contract from the archives: "This is about where you're going."
  • A piece of returned equipment has markings she recognises from a file that should not exist.
  • She asks a party to verify what actually happened on a job, not for the report, for the record.

Kael Orvaine
Field Trainer & Combat Coordinator · half-orc, late 30s
Tap ▾
Runs orientation for new recruits, posts the difficulty ratings on contracts, and is responsible for the sign on the wall that reads "DEAD MEMBERS COMPLETE NO QUESTS." A pragmatist who has made peace with caring.
Personality
Measured, economical with words, will give you exactly the advice you need to not die, once. Does not repeat himself. Has a laugh that is rare and worth earning. Holds a deep professional respect for competence wherever he finds it.
Appearance
Built like a door. Moves like something that has been hurt before and decided to learn from it. One tusk is chipped; he says it is from a training accident, everyone assumes otherwise. Always wears lightweight scale mail, even indoors.
Notable Traits
  • Rates every incoming party within 10 minutes of watching them interact
  • Keeps informal notes on which contract types suit which party compositions
  • Will pull a contract for a group he thinks is not ready yet, and explain exactly why
  • Runs sparring sessions at dawn that anyone can join, no questions asked
Guild Stats
Athletics +9Perception +7Insight +6Survival +7Tactics (Expert)Former Rank 6
⚿ GM Secret
Kael lost a full party on a Head Office sanctioned mission he was told was low-risk. The official report listed it as a field accident. He has the real orders. He kept the job because walking away felt like abandoning the people the guild could still protect. He does not know what he will do if he discovers Aldric knew the mission was compromised.
⚓ Hooks
  • He refuses to rate a new contract, and will not say why until pushed.
  • He asks a senior party to take a rookie along, phrasing it like a favour but meaning it as a test.
  • He approaches a party privately after a debrief: "I need to ask you something. Off the record."

Dova Crestmark
Guildhall Barkeeper · dwarf, appears ageless
Tap ▾
Runs the guildhall's tavern with iron authority and an endless supply of something she calls "the good stuff." Nobody knows her age, her hometown, or how she got the job. Nobody asks twice.
Personality
Blunt, generous in unexpected moments, and possessed of an unsettling ability to know what you need before you say it. Has a philosophy about drinking that sounds like tavern wisdom until you realise it is actually life advice.
Appearance
Short even by dwarf standards, broad-shouldered, with copper-streaked braids and burn scars on both forearms she does not explain. Wears an apron with so many pockets it is practically armour. Laughs like she means it.
Notable Traits
  • Never forgets a drink order or a secret told at her bar
  • Has a complex unofficial credit system that somehow works
  • Can read a room's tension level in seconds and calibrate accordingly
  • Makes a non-alcoholic house brew she gives to people she is worried about
Guild Stats
Perception +8Sleight of Hand +6Brewing (Legendary)Guild Informant
⚿ GM Secret
Dova is technically a guild employee but also a named informant for a separate organisation, not hostile, but parallel. She passes on nothing that harms her regulars. She has, once, passed on something that saved the whole branch without anyone knowing it came from her. The arrangement is old, the details classified even from Aldric.
⚓ Hooks
  • She slides a folded note with someone's drink: "Don't open it here."
  • A traveller sits at the bar and she serves them without being asked what they want, and they do not seem surprised.
  • She asks a trusted party to quietly remove someone from the premises before Aldric sees them.

Pip Sallow
Guildhall Server & Odd-Jobs · halfling, early 20s
Tap ▾
Fastest-moving thing in the guildhall, somehow always where he is needed and almost never where he is not. Enthusiastic to a fault, desperately wants to be an adventurer, and is being gently protected from that ambition by everyone who likes him.
Personality
Bright-eyed, relentlessly cheerful, asks too many questions about what it is like "out there." Has a talent for being invisible when useful and visible when annoying. Genuinely kind. More perceptive than he looks.
Appearance
Small even for a halfling, with unruly brown curls and ink smudges on his wrists from writing down orders he forgets to stop taking. Wears the tavern apron two sizes too big. Always slightly out of breath.
Notable Traits
  • Has memorised everyone's usual order
  • Draws maps of "the adventures he's going to have someday," some disturbingly accurate
  • Has a nose for when people are lying, just does not know what to do with it yet
  • The guildhall cats follow him. All of them.
Guild Stats
Stealth +5Perception +4Persuasion +4Sleight of Hand +3
⚿ GM Secret
Pip is the illegitimate child of a guild Rank 9 operative who does not know he exists. Pip does not know who his parent is. He just knows the guild took him in when he was seven and no one has ever told him the full story. He stopped asking. He has not stopped wondering. One of his "adventure maps" accidentally sketched a location that his missing parent's last mission was centred on.
⚓ Hooks
  • He asks a party to take him "just to the edge of town, just to see," and something actually happens.
  • He brings a party the wrong order, which turns out to be a note slipped under a plate by someone who knew they would be there.
  • He has seen someone hanging around outside the guildhall for three nights and finally tells someone.
