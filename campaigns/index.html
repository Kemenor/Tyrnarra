What This Is

Everything under /campaigns/ is GM material. The player-facing site carries only chronicler-tier and folk-tier canon; the GM-tier mechanisms, NPC secrets, hidden locations, and live plot threads live here, kept out of the player sidebar by design.

Each campaign keeps its own hub. Open one to reach its town notes, location dossiers, and quests.
