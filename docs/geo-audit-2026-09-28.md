# Geography audit: lore against the map (2026-09-28)

A read-only pass over every geography file in `lore/` against the Wonderdraft master map. Ground truth: region and domain polygons plus the land mask (neighbours, compass bearings, Hafra vs Midarra coasts, contained labels), the map preview, and the published `terrain.webp` for painted rivers and lakes (the preview tool does not draw them). Roughly 430 claims checked out; what follows is what did not.

**Seas as the map draws them:** the **Midarra** is the inner sea the ring continent encloses, opening west at Balatur Erui; the **Hafra** is all outer sea (teal on the terrain export, the Midarra deep blue). The **Cloud Sea** is not drawn.

Legend: **A** = lore fix, map clearly right, no creative call. **D** = decision needed (fix the lore or move the map). **T** = already tracked in `map-todo.md`.

## Lore fixes (A), by file

**Applied 2026-09-28** (lore and the published pages that repeat each claim; glossary follow-ups included). The Air Monastery "east of Haizava" fix assumes D1 keeps the Haizava mark where it is.

**Sumendar** (`sumendar.md`)
- :3 Borders lists Floteyn: land borders are Lioaru and Lautara; Floteyn is across the Midarra to the north.
- :5, :9 "Hafra to the south and west": the Hafra coast is west and south-west; the south is the Lioaru land border.
- :5 Midarra coast omits the whole north shore (No Man's Land, the Order's mountain wall).
- :9 "South-southwest Talan": west-southwest.
- :11 the orange-brown ridge walls the Order of Steam off from the rest of Sumendar; a lower brown range marks the Lioaru border.
- :29 the bay's coasts: Tahu Tangata the south, the Dreaming Cape the east and north-east headland.

**Lioaru** (`lioaru.md`)
- :9 "west of Brauogi": Brauogi is across the Midarra, far north; say "west of Ezkudon, south-west of Lautara".
- :5, :9 Hafra coast is south and west (Galdua Jendea's west shore, the Lost Kingdom's west and south).
- :11 vs :25 Lost Kingdom "southern coastal" vs "western-coast": the south-west coast in both.
- :15 Azarketi on "the isles offshore of the Lost Kingdom": none within ~225 mi; name the southern isles (Lost Isle, the Duchies' and Hareaveldi's islands).
- :11 "orange sand dunes": the painted sand is pale; the orange is Sumendar's (cosmetic).

**Myrkono** (`myrkono.md`)
- :5, :9 seas: Hafra to the west and north-west (Three Pines, Ilun Tasun); the Midarra along the whole south coast, Itzasoa to Bikitsa.
- :30 Bikitsa at the **eastern** edge, not the northern.
- :66 "the clean inverse of Lograth across the mountains": Lurrath is across the mountains (or "Lograth across the continent").
- :110 Three Pines "the one Myrkono country with no Wardstone": Bikitsa has none either.
- :104 "even the smallest island runs better than 250 miles": each is ~155–175 mi; the cluster ~250×300.
- :11 "mountain spine through the middle": walls on the east, scattered ranges inland.
- :27 Izarelai is the south-**east**; :56 Myrria in the basin's western reaches; :58 open to the Hafra along the western side.
- :80 Itzasoa faces Balatur Erui across the gate (no land border); the shadow-forest covers the western third, not "the better half"; the Izarelai border river springs from Botbar's lake, not the range.
- :38 Izarelai's neighbours omit Bikitsa (east, behind a mountain wall) and Greenward (north-east).
- :30 "the only cross-domain kingdom on Talan": fine if Lua Lasai and the Basogur (both straddle domains) are not single kingdoms; confirm.
