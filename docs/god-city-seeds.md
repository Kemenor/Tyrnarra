# God-City Seeds

Locked seeds for the **13 Bound-god city-states**, defined together (2026-05-30) so each stays unique. Build them out one at a time via the `sub-region-workflow` skill (full workflow each). The bar is the Merkavar deepening: a god-city is a *seed*, not a name with a byname.

**Process:** full workflow per city, one at a time. **Build order started with Haizava** (Wind), because the god-city pass grew out of the Vindul depth-audit. City *names* are provisional; propose a better-fitting name per god at build time if one exists. The byname is the descriptor.

**Status legend:** *seed locked* = the seed and uniqueness are final, ready for a full-workflow build; *stub* = a placeholder page exists but still needs the full build; *building* = in progress; *done* = lore (and HTML) committed. Every city below that is not yet *done* is seed-locked; Lograth additionally has a stub page awaiting the full build.

## Uniqueness architecture

The 2-sentence test governs: a reader should know which city a description names within two sentences.

- **The elemental quartet** are four answers to *change*: Eldara **forges** (creation through the friction of method-vs-recklessness), Uravel **flows** (adapt or drown), Haizava **is rebuilt by the wind** (read it, do not map it), Lurrath **endures** (refuses to change at all).
- **The make/build/know cluster** (the easiest to blur): Nahaskel **erupts** (mortal madness, undisciplined, ephemeral, nothing lasts); Eldara **builds** (disciplined-by-its-other-half, ships lasting machines); Thekkavar **learns** (knows, teaches, keeps). Three verbs, three domains.
- **Haizava vs Nahaskel** (the sharpest call): Haizava changes because *the wind does it to the city* (elemental, beautiful, beyond control). Nahaskel changes because *its people will not stop doing it to themselves* (mortal, mad, generative). Wind-machine vs mad-workshop.
- **The light-dark pair:** Ljosarn **exposes** (eternal light, nothing hides); Myrria **hides and forgives** (shadow, second chances).
- **Frae City vs Nahaskel** ("no rules" could rhyme): Frae City is *free* (the people are sane and deliberately ungoverned, principled anti-authority); Nahaskel is *mad* (reality itself will not hold still). Liberty vs chaos.
- **Thekkavar vs Azkataria vs Atarialda** (knowledge): Azkataria **argues** it (open-floor agora); Thekkavar **teaches and keeps** it (academy and stacks); Atarialda **remembers** it (oral, in the kitchen, the anti-library). Thekkavar is the formal-institutional pole.
- **The manifestation strata** (locked 2026-06-13): each god walks their city in one of three registers, and a new build picks one deliberately. **Open** (the god as themselves, recognised): Haizava (Fisaya rides svifs) · Frae City (the broken chain names him) · Ljosarn (Iro in his own face, falling in step). **Both registers** (an open face and a hidden one): Merkavar (witnessed rite / veiled customer) · Valreka (elder + adult faces open, the child face secret: Amona) · Thekkavar (openly browsing the stacks, "wandered in by accident from the Infinite one" / the incognito lecturer) · Eldara (the open witness of finished masterworks / the hidden Apprentice). **Hidden** (the stranger): Myrria (the stranger who talks) · Nahaskel (the New Face) · Uravel (the One Who Sits). Lurrath, Lograth, and Veidrath choose at build time.

### Built (lore + HTML) — reference-depth exemplars

- **Haizava** (Wind / Fisaya) — the city the wind physically rebuilds (sails, vanes, rope-bridges; hire a wind-reader, not a map); the one place the Wyndwalken's maps cannot fix. ◈ the Stranger on the Svif; ⚿ Who Banishes the Grasping (the Windwatchers' counter-trim).
- **Eldara** (Fire / Komo) — the dwarf-and-kobold dual-temper sacred forge, making-as-worship, the *almosts* plumb the city; distinct from the Order of Steam (industrial kingdom). ◈ the Apprentice (Komo helps or detonates); ⚿ the mountain has a clock (the Emberwardens' eruption-vigil).
- **Valreka** (Time / Tani) — *formerly "Denbora."* Whale-borne nomad city roaming Galdua Jendea to recover **Oroiri**, Tani's buried holy city scattered when she died; the **mother whale** (alive since the rebirth) bears palace + sanctum + the memory-keepers, lesser whales carry the houses (nobility = whale-guidance), the **Strays** ride **Stokkul**, the **Azarketi** are water-bearer penitents. ◈ the old woman at the dig; ⚿ Amona of the Strays (Tani rides incognito). "Denbora" now names only the Storveldi Denbora empire.
- **Thekkavar** (Knowledge / Enki) — every facet of life learnable (the **Lanterns**), a life is a *descent*, the discipline of the earned answer, closed-doors-as-mercy; the **Convocation** + **Provost**, the **Sealed** clergy, the **Leize** (Infinite Library) beneath. ◈ Enki teaches (incognito lecturer); ⚿ the price in the deep (forget-and-pay-in-years / remember-and-serve) and what waits in the Leize.
- **Frae City** · the Unchained City (Freedom / Cronus) — the first free city, a seven-chained floating rock over the Askamira lake, born as the base of mortal resistance in the Week of Crimson Rain; free-not-lawless (law-by-consent), no master, offices by **lot and short rotation**; house / guild / commoner sit in assembly as equals; the **Common Table**, the **Drawn**, the **Watch**, the **Unbidden**, the **Broken Link**, the **Open Schools**, the **Rim**, the **Seven Chains**. ◈ the broken-chain man (Cronus walks among the free and arm-wrestles); ⚿ Cronus was mortal.
- **Ljosarn** (Light / Iro) — the light-hearth (Icelandic *ljós* + *arinn*): the everbright beacon on **Vonura** (the holy lake, the city-state's whole territory), lit without one dark night through the Lost and Dark Eras (the **Vonarlog** horizon-glow; *walking to the light*); the **flame lifecycle** (family flames + the handmade **Argontzi** built from the city's one melt of every returned lantern, all the city's ancestors in every vessel; the First Carry · leaving-home exchange · **Ezkonsu** · divorce-return · death-melt; all personal fire and metal route through the sanctum) + the **Heimfar** (every Iro church on Talan relays a lantern home); civic fire is pooled mortal fire that never touches the sanctum: district flames hosted in *homes* year by year, the city flame four years (**Ljosarn's Light**), succession by **the Passing** (the walk that is a judging; a wronged district **douses** the flame and relights from its own pooled fire) under *the light is given, never imposed*; night as the second day (the windowing; *dark enough to sleep in*; youth dare the dark rows on the black water); exports lantern-craft, light-rites, healers, **lake-aged** (*sunken summer*) wine; imports the continent's hopeless (hospice-quays; the dread that despair outnumbers the hope); Iro walks **openly as himself**, falling in step on the hard walks, complimenting Argontzi bench-work. ◈ the light he carries (god-praised lanterns; he attends every Passing and never intervenes); ⚿ the dark lantern in the Light-Hearth (the Sun-Champion's scoured lantern; why Iro is silent on zealotry; the Drambur seam). `lore/geography/egulon.md`, *Ljosarn*; page at `egulon/ljosarn/ljosarn.html`.
- **Uravel** (Water / Shuun) — the water-weave (Basque *ur* + Icelandic *vefur*): drifting isles above, dry halls sunk below, **weight is fate** (the light float high, fast, and drown easy; the grown sink deep and slow), tiers sorted by light (**the Open / the Blue / the Dark**); the halls hold their air on Shuun's **blessing-follows-connection** (*a hall no one visits drowns*) or maintained enchantment, so the city has two drownings, water for the shallow and silence for the deep; **Urdeia** the sanctum bell + the daily **Answering** (acoustic census; *Uravel is whatever answers*) + the weekly **Calling** (*to earn your water is to rise from the depths*); locals swim (fitted gills = assimilation), visitors ride bells and glass-drops (dry marks the outsider); mirrored youth dares (air-born dare down, water-born dare dry) + the opposite-keepers; hull-sovereignty, **the Shoal**, **the Linewards**, the tether-rate + posted board; economy "the city that keeps" (deep vaults + deep-aged, fitted gills + bell-craft, the fixed anchorage; forever a season from darkness); **Urbarren** the sanctum at the true bottom + the Listeners' surface post. ◈ the One Who Sits (Shuun seated, listening, never speaking first); ⚿ **Isilun**, the Silence (the drowned quarter beside Urbarren; Shuun sat with it to the end, witnessed, listened, did not save). `lore/geography/floteyn.md`, *Uravel*; page at `floteyn/uravel/uravel.html`.
- **Nahaskel** · the Unmapped City (Chaos / Vesuna) — the city its own people remake overnight (the private notion) while everything *shared* is decided by the open coin-throw, **the Casting**; no standing government, the churn the anti-tyranny safeguard (Lograth's inverse). Pointed navigation-absence (no map, ask-as-you-go) + conveyance-churn (**Grumble**, the bites-when-hungry beast); youth as **thrill-crews** vs **permanence-rebels** (**Dossa**); spark-export economy (**Pim**, the prospectors' idea-fair); **the Tellers** (loose clergy; **Old Tally**). ◈ the New Face (Vesuna as the unknown newcomer with the next idea); ⚿ the coin is honest except at the two edges (anti-tyranny floor + anti-dissolution ceiling, agency-intact, contrast-safe vs Haizava) + **the Unthrown** (the still room: throne, bare chair, un-cast coin; the city is free while the gamble stays in the air). `lore/geography/nashavel.md`, *Nahaskel*; page at `nashavel/nahaskel/nahaskel.html`.
- **Lurrath** · the Steadfast City (Earth / Sarrum) — the bedrock (Basque *lur* + *harri*) inside the **Eraztumen** ring (the ring is the wall + the whole territory; Gotorlekua retired in the swap); **the city that does not fall**, Endurance read as *foundation* not stasis (secure, prosperous, deep-rooted, the place you build on). You are **borne** in: the rail dies at the **Harrate** gate and the **portage** carries everything the last miles (never the hub, by mountain-cost not refusal); the ring grows **Iraungar** (keeping-grain, famine-insurance) and **Iraungardo** (keeping-beer); Lurrath **builds permanence** (the continent's roads/walls/foundations). Moves on the **graven ways** (ramps not stairs) and runs on **divinotech** (Sarrum's power through steadfast faith; most-faithfully-kept runs best; place-bound). **The Naming** (the **Keepers'** dawn liturgy of hundreds of voices; reads every soul still lingering in the Postlife, turns over as souls pass to the Wellspring); government **the Bearers** (climb by carrying, the **First Bearer** carries most); youth: coming-of-age **the ring-climb** (see the world, choose, some go over), rebellion **the sealed deep**. Sarrum is **the one already standing**, reads at the Naming as one steadfast voice. ◈ *he will read you* (the **scroll of the unremembered**); ⚿ **the names of power** (the liturgy unknowingly speaks the demon lords' true mortal names; Sarrum's scroll holds the forgotten seats; Drambur the remembered exception; Lethar the one name it cannot reach). **Zutarri** the sanctum. `lore/geography/brauogi.md`, *Lurrath*; HTML pending.
- **Veidrath** · the Hunting City (Hunt / Hinka) — the hunting-stead (Icelandic *veiðr* + *staðr*; the Icelandic mirror of Basque Ehizahar), the Hunt's seat **caught mid-settling and witnessed live**. Three strata: the ancient sacred **Core** around **Igarbe** (the sanctum; Basque *igaro* "to cross over / to die", folk *the Crossing*) + a growing belt of settled districts (one raised an **airship tower**, one courts the **magirail**) + seasonal **tent-rings**, the stone-frontier advancing each year. The clans are **varied agropastoralists** (herding, seasonal cultivation, craft, trade; the hunt the sacred thread, not the whole economy) on a settle-roam **spectrum** that always existed and is sliding rooted-ward; a **de-synchronized** year of many tempos with peaks; status the kill (old) vs the holding (new). Movement: **circuits** (centrifugal migration-loops + bond-beasts) vs the **towers** (centripetal). Economy: the great wild-to-world **exchange** (furs, bond-beasts, exported hunters/guides; the great hunt as draw) + the who-earns-the-wild's-worth tension. Government: no single rule by design, the **Stillhands** (Hinka's clergy) host the gathering and keep the **Gathering-Peace**, the settled districts grow town-institutions, nothing bridges them. Youth: the **first hunt** (the wild reclaims every child once; the soft-kill argument), the cross-the-spectrum rebellion whose lopsidedness settles the city. **Faith:** Hinka hunted and her sanctum emptied with the city; now she settles with her people, **transforming** from the wild Huntress toward the **Tender of every threshold** (gods don't die, they transform with belief, the **Araphel** precedent), witnessed live; the Stillhands' two vocations (field-priests / death-priests) split over loss-vs-flowering. ◈ the two thresholds (met at the kill and the bedside; *around* more than she was); ⚿ **there is no first Hinka to keep** (the wild Huntress is herself a late belief-form; the conservatives freeze one frame of a goddess always becoming). `lore/geography/ehizahar.md`, *Veidrath*; HTML pending. **Manifestation stratum: open** (Hinka met as herself, the live transformation the page's heart).
