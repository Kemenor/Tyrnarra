# God-City Seeds

Locked seeds for the **13 Bound-god city-states**, defined together (2026-05-30) so each stays unique. Build them out one at a time via the `sub-region-workflow` skill (full workflow each). The bar is the Merkavar deepening: a god-city is a *seed*, not a name with a byname.

**Process:** full workflow per city, one at a time. **Build order started with Haizava** (Wind), because the god-city pass grew out of the Vindul depth-audit. City *names* are provisional; propose a better-fitting name per god at build time if one exists. The byname is the descriptor.

**Status legend:** *seed locked* = the seed and uniqueness are final, ready for a full-workflow build; *building* = in progress; *done* = lore (and HTML) committed. Every unbuilt city below is seed-locked.

## Uniqueness architecture

The 2-sentence test governs: a reader should know which city a description names within two sentences.

- **The elemental quartet** are four answers to *change*: Eldara **forges** (creation through the friction of method-vs-recklessness), Uravel **flows** (adapt or drown), Haizava **is rebuilt by the wind** (read it, do not map it), Lurrath **endures** (refuses to change at all).
- **The make/build/know cluster** (the easiest to blur): Nahaskel **erupts** (mortal madness, undisciplined, ephemeral, nothing lasts); Eldara **builds** (disciplined-by-its-other-half, ships lasting machines); Thekkavar **learns** (knows, teaches, keeps). Three verbs, three domains.
- **Haizava vs Nahaskel** (the sharpest call): Haizava changes because *the wind does it to the city* (elemental, beautiful, beyond control). Nahaskel changes because *its people will not stop doing it to themselves* (mortal, mad, generative). Wind-machine vs mad-workshop.
- **The light-dark pair:** Ljosarn **exposes** (eternal light, nothing hides); Myrria **hides and forgives** (shadow, second chances).
- **Frae City vs Nahaskel** ("no rules" could rhyme): Frae City is *free* (the people are sane and deliberately ungoverned, principled anti-authority); Nahaskel is *mad* (reality itself will not hold still). Liberty vs chaos.
- **Thekkavar vs Azkataria vs Atarialda** (knowledge): Azkataria **argues** it (open-floor agora); Thekkavar **teaches and keeps** it (academy and stacks); Atarialda **remembers** it (oral, in the kitchen, the anti-library). Thekkavar is the formal-institutional pole.

## Per-city build notes

### Still to build

- **Uravel** (Water / Shuun) — a multi-tier archipelago-city: a scatter of islets of wildly uneven size (some a hundred metres, some kilometres long) plus magic-breathable structures sunk between and beneath them, lived in vertical tiers (surface isles + submerged levels). **Movement is the spine** (boat between isles, dive and shaft between tiers; the amphibious peoples move freely up and down). Deliberately heterogeneous and kept open: some isles fixed, some truly drifting; some fixed ones bridged, others reachable only by boat or dive; some breathe on Shuun's standing blessing, some on a maintained enchantment. Seed: adapt or drown, Adaptability the virtue and Drowning the doom. **Open build-questions:** the breathing-magic (Shuun's blessing vs maintained spell, and whether its failure is the city's great dread, the Eldara-eruption / Valreka-mother-whale equivalent); whether and which islets shift. Floteyn peoples: Merfolk, Athamaru, Tripkee.
- **Veidrath** (Hunt / Hinka) — the Hunt's god-seat at the moment the roaming clans meet the settled life. Ulaanbaatar shape: a permanent sacred/administrative core (Hinka's sanctum + the few districts that stay) ringed by clan-quarters that swell and empty with the season, some clans now putting down permanent walls and some refusing. **Central tension:** settle-vs-roam (a settling faction against a roaming one). The seed's edge keeps it predatory: the city is a hunting-ground, status is the kill, patience is law. **Build flag:** read distinct from **Baerfrost** (Vindul's arctic Hunt-League). Baerfrost is the Hunt made a *stable institution* (codified status-sport, skald-record, Hammer-Lord vote, Jotunborn); Veidrath is the Hunt-seat *mid-transformation* (the four Villtur tribes, Orc / Kholo / Lizardfolk / Centaur, gathering at the threshold of settlement). Note the domain already holds settled cultures (**Fenurra**), so settlement is a known pull rather than a novelty; Veidrath is the crucible where the two ways negotiate.
- **Nahaskel** (Chaos / Vesuna) — the city its own people will not stop reinventing (mortal madness and creativity, where Haizava's *wind* does it for them). Perpetual works, always construction; sleep here and the bakery across the street may be a nail-shop by morning. **Nothing is fixed, not even the sanctum.** Wonders *and* abominations (some overnight creations are marvels, some dangerous or monstrous, the Chaos tax), never the same twice. **The people are the only continuity:** the most understanding, least-surprised, most adaptive folk on Talan, perpetually a little lost and at peace with it, embracing new tech, norms, and ideas faster than anyone. **Politics:** the one tension is that the government itself churns week to week, cycling through every form (democracy, autocracy, communism, gynocracy, egalitarianism, and on) as genuine experiments; the churn is the *safeguard*, since no tyrant can entrench before the city reinvents itself out from under him (the inverse of Lograth, whose law is absolute and forever). Navigation: no map and no wind-reader, locals ask-as-they-go and accept the lostness, which floors visitors.
- **Lurrath** (Earth / Sarrum) — the Steadfast City, the unmoved stone. Needs full grilling; sits within Gotorlekua's ring of peaks (Eraztumen), Brauogi, beside the Goblin / Hobgoblin / Minotaur three-way. Endurance and burden are the keys: the stone that has never fallen, never changed, and bears every weight laid on it. Contrast the elemental quartet (it *refuses* to change where Eldara forges, Uravel flows, Haizava is wind-rebuilt).
- **Ljosarn** (Light / Iro) — the Everbright City, the city without shadow: eternal light as hope and as nowhere-to-hide. Needs full grilling; Egulon, Nagaji-and-Shoony, Iro's zealotry aspect and the Drambur GM-wound are the seams to mind. Light-dark pair with Myrria (Ljosarn exposes; Myrria hides and forgives).
- **Lograth** (Law / Forseti) — the Judgment City, the court of last resort, law made flesh and the oath absolute. Needs the deeper dive past its Thousand-Kingdom framing (it sits within the Thousand Kingdom, Zuzental, the reference-implementation of Forseti's law). The permanent-absolute-law foil to Nahaskel's churn.

- **Eldara** (Fire / Komo) — the dwarf-and-kobold dual-temper sacred forge, making-as-worship, the *almosts* plumb the city; distinct from the Order of Steam (industrial kingdom). ◈ the Apprentice (Komo helps or detonates); ⚿ the mountain has a clock (the Emberwardens' eruption-vigil).
- **Valreka** (Time / Tani) — *formerly "Denbora."* Whale-borne nomad city roaming Galdua Jendea to recover **Oroiri**, Tani's buried holy city scattered when she died; the **mother whale** (alive since the rebirth) bears palace + sanctum + the memory-keepers, lesser whales carry the houses (nobility = whale-guidance), the **Strays** ride **Stokkul**, the **Azarketi** are water-bearer penitents. ◈ the old woman at the dig; ⚿ Amona of the Strays (Tani rides incognito). "Denbora" now names only the Storveldi Denbora empire.
- **Thekkavar** (Knowledge / Enki) — every facet of life learnable (the **Lanterns**), a life is a *descent*, the discipline of the earned answer, closed-doors-as-mercy; the **Convocation** + **Provost**, the **Sealed** clergy, the **Leize** (Infinite Library) beneath. ◈ Enki teaches (incognito lecturer); ⚿ the price in the deep (forget-and-pay-in-years / remember-and-serve) and what waits in the Leize.
- **Frae City** · the Unchained City (Freedom / Cronus) — the first free city, a seven-chained floating rock over the Askamira lake, born as the base of mortal resistance in the Week of Crimson Rain; free-not-lawless (law-by-consent), no master, offices by **lot and short rotation**; house / guild / commoner sit in assembly as equals; the **Common Table**, the **Drawn**, the **Watch**, the **Unbidden**, the **Broken Link**, the **Open Schools**, the **Rim**, the **Seven Chains**. ◈ the broken-chain man (Cronus walks among the free and arm-wrestles); ⚿ Cronus was mortal.
