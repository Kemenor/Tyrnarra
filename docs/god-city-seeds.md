# God-City Seeds

Locked seeds for the **13 Bound-god city-states**, defined together (2026-05-30) so each stays unique. Build them out one at a time via the `sub-region-workflow` skill (full workflow each). The bar is the Merkavar deepening: a god-city is a *seed*, not a name with a byname.

**Process:** full workflow per city, one at a time. **Build order started with Haizava** (Wind), because the god-city pass grew out of the Vindul depth-audit. City *names* are provisional; propose a better-fitting name per god at build time if one exists. The byname is the descriptor.

**Status legend:** *seed locked* = the seed and uniqueness are final, ready for a full-workflow build; *stub* = a placeholder page exists but still needs the full build; *building* = in progress; *done* = lore (and HTML) committed. Every city below that is not yet *done* is seed-locked; Lograth additionally has a stub page awaiting the full build.

## Uniqueness architecture

The 2-sentence test governs: a reader should know which city a description names within two sentences.

- **The elemental quartet** are four answers to *change*: Eldara **forges** (creation through the friction of method-vs-recklessness), Uravel **flows** (adapt or drown), Haizava **is rebuilt by the wind** (read it, do not map it), Lurrath **endures** (refuses to change at all).
- **The make/build/know cluster** (the easiest to blur): Nahaskel **erupts** (mortal madness, undisciplined, ephemeral, nothing lasts); Eldara **builds** (disciplined-by-its-other-half, ships lasting machines); Thekkavar **learns** (knows, teaches, keeps). Three verbs, three domains.
- **Haizava vs Nahaskel** (the sharpest call): Haizava changes because *the wind does it to the city* (elemental, beautiful, beyond control). Nahaskel changes because *its people will not stop doing it to themselves* (mortal, mad, generative). Wind-machine vs mad-workshop.
- **The light-dark pair:** Ljosarn **exposes** (eternal light, nothing hides); Myrria **hides and forgives** (shadow, second chances).
- **Frae City vs Nahaskel** ("no rules" could rhyme): Frae City is *free* (the people are sane and deliberately ungoverned, principled anti-authority); Nahaskel is *mad* (reality itself will not hold still). Liberty vs chaos.
- **Thekkavar vs Azkataria vs Atarialda** (knowledge): Azkataria **argues** it (open-floor agora); Thekkavar **teaches and keeps** it (academy and stacks); Atarialda **remembers** it (oral, in the kitchen, the anti-library). Thekkavar is the formal-institutional pole.
- **The manifestation strata** (locked 2026-06-13): each god walks their city in one of three registers, and a new build picks one deliberately. **Open** (the god as themselves, recognised): Haizava (Fisaya rides svifs) · Frae City (the broken chain names him) · Ljosarn (Iro in his own face, falling in step). **Both registers** (an open face and a hidden one): Merkavar (witnessed rite / veiled customer) · Valreka (elder + adult faces open, the child face secret: Amona) · Thekkavar (openly browsing the stacks, "wandered in by accident from the Infinite one" / the incognito lecturer) · Eldara (the open witness of finished masterworks / the hidden Apprentice). **Hidden** (the stranger): Myrria (the stranger who talks) · Nahaskel (the New Face) · Uravel (the One Who Sits). Lurrath, Lograth, and Veidrath choose at build time.

## Per-city build notes

### Still to build

- **Veidrath** (Hunt / Hinka) — the Hunt's god-seat at the moment the roaming clans meet the settled life. Ulaanbaatar shape: a permanent sacred/administrative core (Hinka's sanctum + the few districts that stay) ringed by clan-quarters that swell and empty with the season, some clans now putting down permanent walls and some refusing. **Central tension:** settle-vs-roam (a settling faction against a roaming one). The seed's edge keeps it predatory: the city is a hunting-ground, status is the kill, patience is law. **Build flag:** read distinct from **Baerfrost** (Vindul's arctic Hunt-League). Baerfrost is the Hunt made a *stable institution* (codified status-sport, skald-record, Hammer-Lord vote, Jotunborn); Veidrath is the Hunt-seat *mid-transformation* (the Villtur peoples, Orc / Lizardfolk / Goloma, gathering at the threshold of settlement). Note the domain already holds settled cultures (**Fenurra**), so settlement is a known pull rather than a novelty; Veidrath is the crucible where the two ways negotiate.
- **Lurrath** (Earth / Sarrum) — the Steadfast City, the unmoved stone. Needs full grilling; sits within Gotorlekua's ring of peaks (Eraztumen), Brauogi, beside the Minotaur / Kholo / Dragonet three-way. Endurance and burden are the keys: the stone that has never fallen, never changed, and bears every weight laid on it. Contrast the elemental quartet (it *refuses* to change where Eldara forges, Uravel flows, Haizava is wind-rebuilt).
- **Lograth** (Law / Forseti) — the Judgment City, the court of last resort, law made flesh and the oath absolute. Needs the deeper dive past its Thousand-Kingdom framing (it sits within the Thousand Kingdom, Zuzental, the reference-implementation of Forseti's law). The permanent-absolute-law foil to Nahaskel's churn. **A stub page already exists** at `/setting/talan/domains/zuzental/lograth/lograth.html` (it carries the canonical *the Goddess judges, she does not write* theology); the build is fleshing it out past the stub, not starting from scratch.

- **Ljosarn** (Light / Iro) — the light-hearth (Icelandic *ljós* + *arinn*): the everbright beacon on **Vonura** (the holy lake, the city-state's whole territory), lit without one dark night through the Lost and Dark Eras (the **Vonarlog** horizon-glow; *walking to the light*); the **flame lifecycle** (family flames + the handmade **Argontzi** built from the city's one melt of every returned lantern, all the city's ancestors in every vessel; the First Carry · leaving-home exchange · **Ezkonsu** · divorce-return · death-melt; all personal fire and metal route through the sanctum) + the **Heimfar** (every Iro church on Talan relays a lantern home); civic fire is pooled mortal fire that never touches the sanctum: district flames hosted in *homes* year by year, the city flame four years (**Ljosarn's Light**), succession by **the Passing** (the walk that is a judging; a wronged district **douses** the flame and relights from its own pooled fire) under *the light is given, never imposed*; night as the second day (the windowing; *dark enough to sleep in*; youth dare the dark rows on the black water); exports lantern-craft, light-rites, healers, **lake-aged** (*sunken summer*) wine; imports the continent's hopeless (hospice-quays; the dread that despair outnumbers the hope); Iro walks **openly as himself**, falling in step on the hard walks, complimenting Argontzi bench-work. ◈ the light he carries (god-praised lanterns; he attends every Passing and never intervenes); ⚿ the dark lantern in the Light-Hearth (the Sun-Champion's scoured lantern; why Iro is silent on zealotry; the Drambur seam). `lore/geography/egulon.md`, *Ljosarn*; HTML pending.
- **Uravel** (Water / Shuun) — the water-weave (Basque *ur* + Icelandic *vefur*): drifting isles above, dry halls sunk below, **weight is fate** (the light float high, fast, and drown easy; the grown sink deep and slow), tiers sorted by light (**the Open / the Blue / the Dark**); the halls hold their air on Shuun's **blessing-follows-connection** (*a hall no one visits drowns*) or maintained enchantment, so the city has two drownings, water for the shallow and silence for the deep; **Urdeia** the sanctum bell + the daily **Answering** (acoustic census; *Uravel is whatever answers*) + the weekly **Calling** (*to earn your water is to rise from the depths*); locals swim (fitted gills = assimilation), visitors ride bells and glass-drops (dry marks the outsider); mirrored youth dares (air-born dare down, water-born dare dry) + the opposite-keepers; hull-sovereignty, **the Shoal**, **the Linewards**, the tether-rate + posted board; economy "the city that keeps" (deep vaults + deep-aged, fitted gills + bell-craft, the fixed anchorage; forever a season from darkness); **Urbarren** the sanctum at the true bottom + the Listeners' surface post. ◈ the One Who Sits (Shuun seated, listening, never speaking first); ⚿ **Isilun**, the Silence (the drowned quarter beside Urbarren; Shuun sat with it to the end, witnessed, listened, did not save). `lore/geography/floteyn.md`, *Uravel*; page at `floteyn/uravel/uravel.html`.
- **Nahaskel** · the Unmapped City (Chaos / Vesuna) — the city its own people remake overnight (the private notion) while everything *shared* is decided by the open coin-throw, **the Casting**; no standing government, the churn the anti-tyranny safeguard (Lograth's inverse). Pointed navigation-absence (no map, ask-as-you-go) + conveyance-churn (**Grumble**, the bites-when-hungry beast); youth as **thrill-crews** vs **permanence-rebels** (**Dossa**); spark-export economy (**Pim**, the prospectors' idea-fair); **the Tellers** (loose clergy; **Old Tally**). ◈ the New Face (Vesuna as the unknown newcomer with the next idea); ⚿ the coin is honest except at the two edges (anti-tyranny floor + anti-dissolution ceiling, agency-intact, contrast-safe vs Haizava) + **the Unthrown** (the still room: throne, bare chair, un-cast coin; the city is free while the gamble stays in the air). `lore/geography/nashavel.md`, *Nahaskel*; page at `nashavel/nahaskel/nahaskel.html`.
