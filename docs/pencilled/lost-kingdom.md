## Phase 0: settled facts

- **A rework.** Built as a *site* and published (`published/setting/talan/domains/lioaru/lost-kingdom.html`; `lore/geography/lioaru.md`, *Lost Kingdom*), never as a living region. The page: At a Glance, the Guild's standing notice, the six signs of the curse, why the land is this way, the ancestries (Fleshwarp, Skeleton, Duskwalkers), a Lost Isle section, the visitors; four ◈ (the Old Race, the inheritors, the pilgrims' voice, meat on a stick); one ⚿ (the Storveldi Denbora's real history); an open record (the cursed-born city, the inner stone ring, the deep ruins, Lost Isle).
- **Map:** about 57,900 sq mi (~360 × 290 mi), a black triangle on the south-west coast. Its one land neighbour is **Galdua Jendea**, beyond a dark ridge along the north-east edge, past which lies Galdua's pale band (Tazrut, the legions). The rest is Hafra coast; the River Duchies do not touch it. One icon, a ruined city at ~(1792, 7095) on the west of the interior: the Storveldi capital. The Lost Isle ~450 mi WNW, also drawn black (repaint queued).
- **Canon read end to end:** Tani's death, the Storveldi Denbora, Betibizi, integration, the Azarketi (`timeline.md`, `gods.md`, `cosmology.md`, `ancestries.md`); the Corrupted God is the Elden (⚿ `cosmology.md`); the Nine Generals (`factions.md`). The **Rot-Tyrant** is earmarked here, its site to land at this build ("corpse-warlord in ruined armour", "rusting legions"); possible tie: the Storveldi capital stands on Elden ruins. Today's Galdua canon: the ruins read newer because Tani's death keeps them (⚿ tagnit); Tazrut emptied by the cursed ground's spawn in the Dark Era; Ghoran wardens on the edge with warning-cairns; the legions across the pale band since 2524. The Guild's standing warning is signed by **Amestan Anzar**, Valreka's Guild Sovereign (`lioaru.md`). Language: the oldest Iranian form (Avestan / Old Persian sound) survives among those who live in the cursed land; the descent to Hareaveldi's Persian is ⚿ (`_continent.md`); who speaks it is this build's.
- **Stale lines and flags:** "Three and a half thousand years" on the page (it is ~2,500); the page's **Lost Isle** section and open-record line, against the GM's ruling (below); the Azarketi distribution's "around the islands offshore of the Lost Kingdom"; "undeath has never once taken one" (page and the Duskwalker entry; banned phrase); `lioaru.md` calls meat-on-a-stick "canonical truth" in open lore while the page treats it as a trope (if true, behind a ⚿); authorial lines in the page's open record; the Guild rank detail (Silver ceiling, Platinum floor) stands unless changed.
- **Ancestries:** Fleshwarp feeling-first (the Lost Kingdom its full-expression home); Skeleton feeling-first, with reception politics (kingdoms refuse; the Voroir Daua hunt émigrés, leave natives alone), left; Azarketi the offshore-islands line only.
- **GM ruling (2026-10-04): the Lost Isle has no connection to the Lost Kingdom any more and may be renamed.**
- **Tracking:** `open-threads.md` (the cursed-born city: existence decided, all else open; the census line; the Storveldi tongue's speakers); `docs/pencilled/language-zones.md` (three points to settle); the seed bank leads Phase 1.
- **Badges:** none on `lost-kingdom.html`, `storveldi-denbora.html`, `the-binding.html`, `lioaru.html`.
