## Phase 0: settled facts

- **Stub, not a rework.** Dropped to "to be defined" on 2026-09-28 (`58f3b99`). The dropped stub lived only on the Lost Kingdom page: the isle "sharing the curse at lower intensity", its deep history left open (an outpost of the perished kingdom, or an older scar the kingdom built next to). Retired by the GM's ruling (2026-10-04): no tie to the Lost Kingdom; the name is open.
- **The seed** (`seed-bank.md`, GM 2026-10-02 to 10-05): an ordinary port city where every ship arrives; three roads inland, each to an old volcano with a crater lake and a town or city around it; one basin rains gold, one silver, one starlight, the streets run with it, the locals catch it and it leaves through the port. **The Azarketi heartland** (earmarked in `ancestries.md`).
- **Map:** ~130 × 122 mi, near round, ~11,900 sq mi (snapshot area 47,685 px). Painted black; one **large city icon at the centre** (~1053, 6676). Far out in the Hafra: the Red Dominion's capital point ~285 mi NE (nearest land), Galdua's west shore ~330 mi E, the Lost Kingdom's western tip ~310 mi ESE (Ida ~580 mi); **the Cloud Sea's edge ~240 mi W**, the outermost land in the south-western Hafra. About two days' sail by saltkeel (120–180 mi/day) from any coast. Density rule: held heartland 20–40/sq mi gives ~240,000–480,000.
- **Canon to build on:** Lioaru is Time; Tani's three tenses (the Azarketi *answer it now*); the Azarketi feeling (the inherited Elden-blood claim worn as daily pride); ⚿ Storveldi descent, and the "Storveldi-descended lineages" holding partial copies of the integration procedure (`cosmology.md`); the tongue open (Iranian or a Berber sister, GM: either); the Guild's reach from Valreka's Sovereign (Amestan Anzar), the Post's route unwritten. **Tyrnarra has no silver moon**: Solyra the golden sun, Veyru red, Unaru green; the Cloud Sea is luminous white and swallows sun and moons each cycle.
- **Sameness to watch:** the Crimson Rain (⚿ fell where divine deaths concentrated; godblood ground is a shard source); Stillpools (residual Wellspring water-sites, Reflections); Fenurra (a meteor crater ringed by volcanism, with star-metals); Burdineyja's light in water, Star Island, Myrria's Starlight Cliffs; Haraour Eliza and Lurrath basins; Lioaru's harvested treasures (Hareaveldi's arghavan, dived for by Azarketi; Galdua's tagnit; the Lost Kingdom's moment-holding objects); Askamira's threes.
- **Siblings:** built: Valreka, Galdua Jendea, the Lost Kingdom, Hareaveldi. Stub with seed: the River Duchies. Nearest across the water: the Red Dominion (Sumendar; stub with seed, the seafaring branch, a fleet come ashore).
