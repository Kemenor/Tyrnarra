# Dea Elurra (Askamira): working record

**Working record: not canon.** Phases 0–2 were run jointly for the three Askamira countries; read [`askamira.md`](askamira.md) first, including its **Island frame** (shared items any of the three builds may settle for all).

**Current phase:** 3 (traveller's image), open.

---

## Carried from the joint build (Phases 0–2, closed)

- **Map:** ~72,500 sq mi of ice, the island's north; capital icon on the north coast (4475, 3198); the white grades into forest in the north-east; borders Basamortua along the west river and Maitagarri along the north-east river; its southern edge reaches Frae City's lake.
- **Phase 1:** Dea Elurra sings with its shifting ice at dusk and dawn, when the ice sings loudest, the people's music pitched to it. Its aspect of Cronus is **Wandering**: the land moves and its song moves with it.
- **Phase 2 seed (D1):** *The ice country creeps outward and breaks off wherever it meets water (into its own lagoons, into Frae City's lake, into the Midarra), and the bergs drift off singing as they melt.*
- **Island frame, settled:** the rivers flow inward, into the lake; bergs calved into the lake stay there, river ice rides in toward it. Frae City's lake carries Dea Elurra's bergs (approved canon move).
