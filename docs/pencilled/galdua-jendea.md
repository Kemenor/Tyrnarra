## Phase 0: settled facts

- **Stub, not a rework.** Dropped to "to be defined" on 2026-09-28 (`58f3b99`). The dropped stub said: houses Valreka, the whales roam within it; the name Basque, meaning disputed ("Place of Many Waters" or "Place of the Lost People"); the northern corner touches Emarrea, where Heartcourt envoys meet Tani's clergy. Valreka (the god-city) is fully built and published, with the Whalehall.
- **Map:** about 344,000 sq mi (bbox ~760 × 785 mi), one of Talan's largest regions. Open dune sea with four orange dune fields; a pale bleached band along the south-west against the Blackened Lands; Valreka's icon the only one. **No mountains drawn inside**: the only high ground is the brown badland ridge on the north-west (the Sumendar/Lioaru line, No Man's Land's side, where the Cold Furnace stands) and the grey peaks at the northern tip. No rail, no roads in canon.
- **Neighbours:** No Man's Land (north-west, along the brown ridge; the Cold Furnace and the bindstone rush); Tahu Tangata (north-east, across the great river; Valreka buys grain and oil at the river); Emarrea (east corner; envoys when the herd drifts near); Hareaveldi (east, along the river that runs down to the Duchies and carries Emarrea's downstream trade); the River Duchies (south); the Lost Kingdom (south-west); the Hafra (west coast and a short south shore); the Lost Isle offshore west. **Haraour Eliza does not border** (No Man's Land between).
- **Siblings:** built: Valreka. Published, partial: the Lost Kingdom (the cursed-born city open; the Rot-Tyrant earmarked). Stubs with seeds: the River Duchies, Hareaveldi, the Lost Isle.
- **Canon the seed meets:**
  - "The sand that took Oroiri would swallow anything built on it" (`lioaru.md`, *Valreka*): the hanging cities fit it (built on rock). **Why the faithful took to the whales anyway (GM): to find and unearth Oroiri**, which the sand scattered; a city on the rock could not follow it.
  - "Valreka is the one thing that crosses Galdua Jendea entire… the moving market that reaches where nothing fixed can" (`lioaru.md`, *What the roaming city trades*): the gliding caravans meet this line. **Open; probably a canon move** (GM).
  - Galdua Jendea is the **Ghoran heartland** (`lioaru.md`, *Peoples*): this is their full-expression home. The Ghoran are plant-rooted, and the hanging cities hide from the sun: **the GM's answer: the desert sun is too much sun even for a plant; shade-growing has precedent** (German *Nachtschattengewächse*, "night-shade plants").
  - Naming: the open thread (`open-threads.md`) asks for Galdua Jendea's own personal-name structure over the shared Tamazight base, and the local tongue Valreka then speaks.
  - The name: Basque *galdu* (lost) + *jende* (people) reads plainly as "the lost people"; "Many Waters" has no Basque root. A Court Tongue name, so given in the Gods' Era or at 1 MR.
  - Water: the Azarketi water-map of seeps and sand-springs is canon; water is Valreka's one need that never eases.
- **Ancestry fixes:** **Ghoran** carries a vocation ("Many serve as wardens of temporally unstable sites along the Lost Kingdom's edge"), which belongs to the Lost Kingdom; it has no "In Galdua Jendea" full-expression line yet. **Azarketi**: "Valreka's community answers with water" inside the feeling and "cluster on the southern coast" are minor place-matter. **Nagaji**: feeling-only.
- **Flags for other builds:** `lost-kingdom.html` says the Lost Isle "shares the cursed-ground character… No ferry runs there", against its new seed (the Lost Isle's build). `deepening-ideas.md`, *Lautara/Lioaru cultural interface*, assumes "indigenous desert-nomad culture" for Lioaru.
- **Badges:** none on the Lioaru, Valreka, Lost Kingdom, Tahu Tangata or Emarrea pages.
- **Map-todo (GM):** the rock mountains are queued; sites settle at Phase 4.
