# Askamira: the three island polities

**Working record: not canon.** Decisions recorded here are approvals in progress for the build of Basamortua, Dea Elurra and Maitagarri (Askamira). Nothing here is canon until Phase 11 writes it to `lore/`.

**Scope (GM, 2026-10-07):** the three polities are built **together, phase by phase**, each phase answered for all three side by side. **Star Island** is a separate, later build with its own seed (`seed-bank.md`, *Star Island*).

## Phase 0: Canon read (closed 2026-10-07)

**Stub or rework:** all three are stubs (one-line etymologies only, never richer in git). Frae City is the only built place on the island.

**Fixed constraints:**
- Island domain, no land borders; Midarra only; no dungeon of the Nine.
- Three polities, one per biome (GM, 2026-09-28): ice north, desert west, forest east; the climates are uncaused in canon.
- Family: **Dravidian**, the island's own people, there before Frae City (`_continent.md`). Maitagarri Malayalam, Basamortua Telugu, Dea Elurra Kannada, a Tamil core. Frae City and Star Island are native Talanese.
- Region names are Court Tongue (Basque): names of the 1 MR hinge. Self-names in the regional register open.
- Polities default to a Dark-Era founding; the people are older.
- Frae City: 1.1M (350k on the rock, 750k on the shore around the seven anchor-points); Council venue; airship port; on the Solkai to Rika Tikur stillship crossing.
- Cronus's mortal origin is GM-tier; making him an islander would be a canon move.

**Seed bank (GM, 2026-10-02):** the singing island. Dea Elurra: the land (ice) sings. Basamortua: objects sing (crystals in the sand). Maitagarri: the animals sing; bard-led, music as law, the crown to the best-loved voice. In all three the people answer the song.
