## 1. Dwarf

**Proposed entry:**

> **Dominant in Sumendar (Fire / Komo); heartland the Order of Steam.** The methodical people: a Dwarf believes any problem yields to the right procedure, measure, sequence, test, correct, the schematic before the hammer and the checklist after it. A dwarven masterpiece is never a stroke of genius; it is ten thousand corrected errors, and the Dwarf is prouder of that than genius could ever make them.
>
> **In the Order of Steam** (the full-expression home): the New Age Dwarves, lawful, inventive and safety-conscious, builders of airships, steam machinery and arcane artillery; **House Eisenhart** is their highest-prestige lineage (see `geography/sumendar.md`, *Order of Steam → House Eisenhart*).
>
> **In Eldara:** the method-temper without the Order's rulebook, the steady hand that chose to stand beside the explosion, often a dwarf who left the Order or refused it (see `geography/sumendar.md`, *Eldara*).
>
> **In Rika Tikur:** the Order's freight-factors, a standing enclave beside the kobold factors on the processor's doorstep, in perpetual tension with the plutocrat trade-houses (see `geography/lautara.md`, *Rika Tikur*).
>
> **In the upper Darklands:** the **Sentinel Dwarves**, who hold the deep against the hordes that rise from below and set themselves to mend what the failed Golden Empire broke; the most reliable mortal early warning against Corrupted-God leakage, and the Adventurers' Guild treats their intelligence as gold.
>
> **In Fenurra:** the **Draconis** tribe, Mantle-holders of the Scar of Aeris and sole forgers of Blackglass, the method-temper under Dragonblood scale (see `geography/ehizahar/fenurra.md`).

**What changed:** the "two coexisting cultures" framing goes; each culture becomes its place's line. The Sentinel Dwarves have no place file (the Darklands have none), so their line stays here.

## 7. Surki

**Proposed entry** (feeling and the swarm contrast unchanged; the Woven move to their place):

> **Insectoid sapients, and never alone.** A Surki is what they choose, never what they wear, and what Surki choose, almost always, is each other: they think in *we*, work is braided, grief is shared out until each carries a bearable piece, and a Surki speaking of herself slips into the plural within three sentences. The *we* is woven, never granted, a stranger fed the day they arrive and called *we* years later, and once woven in, never dropped. They are the fiercest enemies of the corrupted swarm they are forever mistaken for, because they know better than anyone the difference between a multitude and a hive: the swarm is one will wearing ten thousand bodies, and the Surki are ten thousand wills choosing the same thing. **Dominant in Myrkono (Darkness / Araphel); heartland Itzasoa.** Myrkono is the one domain that takes them in: Araphel's darkness is the quiet and the cover, not a thing to fear. Surface bands elsewhere on Talan are small woven knots who left for reasons of their own; their compound eyes and chitin make them recognisable but not always welcomed in cities that lack Myrkono's habit of welcome.
>
> **In Itzasoa** (the full-expression home): **the Woven**, the wood-wrights who grow the dark-yew *ilunagin* hard as metal and braid the *we* the same slow way, and who fight the swarm most militantly at the **Vermin Queen's** Hollow, which breeds in their own deep wood (see `geography/myrkono.md`, *Itzasoa, the Woven Wood*; `factions.md`, *Remnants of Corruption → The Nine Generals*).
>
> **In Three Pines:** the quick-timber Woven, a standing scandal to their Itzasoa kin: crews braided in months, hulls of isiler pine grown in mere decades, on the argument that a crew is a *we* woven for a voyage (see `geography/myrkono.md`, *Three Pines, the Forest That Sails*).

## 8. Vanara

**Proposed entry:**

> **Dominant in Nashavel (Chaos / Vesuna); heartland the Basogur Jungle**, which straddles Nashavel and Ehizahar. The ones who ride the change: change is the element they are native to. Neither seeking the current nor resisting it, sure-footed on ground that will not hold still; where others need the world fixed or need it changing, a Vanara needs only to know which way it is moving today.
>
> **In the Basogur** (the full-expression home): the guide-clans of the jungle's paths, each walking its own stretch and handing a party on to the next, reading which way the ground moved in the night; the Marutar and Kapisar guide the long road between the web-holds, and the politics of the two domains around the jungle are something they navigate rather than belong to. A Vanara's third name is given by the first travelers she brings through, in their tongue (see `geography/nashavel/basogur.md`).

**Cut (rule 9):** "the way the Strix are native to moving air".
**Receiving file:** `basogur.md`, the Vanara paragraph, gains: "The politics of Nashavel and Ehizahar around the jungle are something the clans navigate rather than belong to."
