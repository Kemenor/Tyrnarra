## Cross-cutting findings

1. **Naming is the gap in nearly every region built before October.** Most regions predate *The families of Talan*: no personal-name structure, no register row, no *What a … is called* section, no named tongue. The recurring stratum fault is Icelandic (the Silent Tongue) on things named long after the Elden, and Basque or Talanese on Dark-Era foundings that should carry the regional register. Several word-bases contradict their assigned family (Myrkono Quechuan, Lautara Japonic, Thekkavar Greek, Vernua Celtic, Kaosadaemi Germanic; Legea and Fenurra superseded). Vindul's tongues are tied to ancestries, against *language follows culture*.
2. **The newer building blocks are missing wholesale from the early builds:** traveller's image, Voices lists, named figures, a population checked against the density rule, dated founding, and the youth block. The god-cities (Myrria, Thekkavar, Lograth, Uravel, Lurrath, Haizava, Eldara, Nahaskel) mostly lack a live tension and a government that says who receives an envoy or signs a treaty.
3. **Anchor ancestries still carry place content.** Not feeling-only: Dwarf (the Order's trades, Rika Tikur and Eldara cultures, the Sentinel Dwarves), Minotaur, Dragonet, Jotunborn, Surki, Ratfolk, Shisk, Vanara (its Basogur home). One sweep of `ancestries.md` clears this for every later redo.
4. **Sameness across regions:** Lurrath and Greenward share the feel-the-ground lantern dare (Baratalda has a third night dare); Lurrath and Haldmark share the "N words" deathbed comfort and offices "climbed by bearing"; Ilun Tasun is a fourth government by lot; every Myrkono region ends on the same "Pressure of the open era" section; Uravel and Balatur Erui share verbatim lines; Floteyn/Egulon carry three one-sentence constitutions and two flame-carried successions.
5. **Prose drift** in the early builds: unattributed wit, epigram closers, "never once / never quite", name-glossing in narration, paragraphs arguing how a region differs from others, and long single bullets.
6. **The south-west word-bases are near-verbatim real words** (Ida's names are attested Persian historical names). CLAUDE.md says inspiration, never a transcription: a GM ruling on whether a drift pass is owed.
