## Phase 0: settled facts

- **Stub, not a rework.** Dropped to "to be defined" on 2026-09-28 (`58f3b99`). The dropped stub: "fully within Lioaru… name predates Tani's influence… **The Nagaji heartland: the serpent-kingdom of the desert**… an old kingdom whose name and identity are older than the cult it now carries… the desert is the great stripper-away, the land that scours the old skin off everything, and Hareaveldi is the kingdom of becoming." Survives: the Nagaji heartland; the name older than Tani; "the serpent-kingdom of the desert" (`ancestries.md`, the registrar).
- **Map:** ~334,000 sq mi mainland plus an island group of ~16,000 sq mi to the south (three wooded islands, one region), ~350,000 in all. Dune sea with dune fields; **two thin rivers** north to south from the border river to the coast; **one green oasis patch on the south coast** with the only icon, a large city at ~(3850, 7490), the unnamed capital. **Neighbours:** north, **Emarrea** across the big border river (Yukihime-no-mori's blossom lowlands on the bank; Biozuri near the north-west corner); west, **Galdua Jendea** and **the River Duchies** along the river that carries Emarrea's downstream trade; east, **the Wildreach** (Ezkudon; seed: the rainbow fey forest, Fey in disguise, a fey lord); south-east corner, probably just touching **the Golden Coast** (Ezkudon; seed: the glass country); south, the Hafra.
- **Canon to build on:** the Nagaji (feeling-only: "shed, and whole again"; Valreka's wrights; Galdua's cliff-builders); Tani's three tenses (the Nagaji are *what's next*); the language ⚿ (Hareaveldi's Persian is the daughter of the Storveldi tongue; Hizva in Ida its oldest form; open prose: scholars argue the likeness); the seed (the painting culture: *what we show, what is real, what is true*; the Persian miniature; sameness flagged with Emarrea's chosen reveal); Emarrea's downstream trade (sake, orchard goods, illusion exports) along Hareaveldi's western edge to the Duchies; Atarialda's kitchen keeps "a Hareaveldi desert-bread".
- **Flags:** (1) the name: the glossary's "deep-old name a pre-Tani people gave" against the strata rule (Basque *harea* + Icelandic *veldi* reads as the gods renaming Elden ground); (2) rail: `lioaru.html` says "Lines reach the River Duchies and Hareaveldi from the wider southern network", `transport.md` names no line; (3) "the serpent-kingdom of the desert" presumes a kingdom; (4) `deepening-ideas.md` assumes "indigenous desert-nomad culture"; (5) an em-dash in `lioaru.html`'s Peoples line; (6) sameness candidates for the seed: Emarrea's chosen reveal and theatre, the Golden Coast seed's glass that shows a past, Thekkavar's Lanterns, Ida's uncertain memory.
- **Siblings:** built: Valreka, Galdua Jendea, the Lost Kingdom. Stubs: the River Duchies; the Lost Isle (Azarketi earmark). Outside Lioaru: Emarrea built; the Wildreach and the Golden Coast stubs with seeds.
- **Badges:** none on the Lioaru, Emarrea or Ezkudon pages.

**GM idea at the readback (2026-10-05): a desert of its own.** Change the terrain and the map: **green oases throughout**, painterly green and colourful spots across the sandy desert, and **the capital five oases together**. On the map-todo; sites at Phase 4. *Sameness to watch:* the River Duchies' seed is "the oasis in the desert… an oasis the size of a kingdom" (one great river-oasis); Hareaveldi's are many small, scattered, colourful oases. Galdua's water is springs inside the rock.
