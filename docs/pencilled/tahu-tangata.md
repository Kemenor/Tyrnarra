## Phase 0: settled facts

- **Stub, not a rework.** Canon held only "old name, meaning partially lost". Its history: first written 2026-05-14 as "the indigenous culture of the volcanic coast" (a domain-card line, with the Order of Steam and No Man's Land as "the southern volcanic band"); a May-2026 to-do note named "Tahu Tangata land-rooted kinship" for Emarrea's neighbour notes; docs of late May listed it as Sumendar's people-and-tradition anchor, though no culture text was ever written; positional detail followed (the bay's south-west arc, the Markaa border); dropped to "to be defined" 2026-09-28. An empty World Anvil entry (Kingdoms, WIP, 2023) existed. The "volcanic coast" reading does not match the map (a plain; the volcanoes lie west in the Red Dominion).
- **Constraints:** Eldara's line runs Eldara → Tahu Tangata → Emarrea → Merkavar, climbing out by the Neck (`transport.md`, `no-mans-land.md`); "most of Eldara's food comes by train from Tahu Tangata and beyond"; the south arc of the Order of Steam bay is its coast (Rika Tikur in the bay, the Order west, the Cape east); the Dreaming Cape's canon cites "holy bay-water remembered in Tahu Tangata songs" (published on the Cape page); the core of the Sumendar family (Māori base, inland, not seafaring); imperial ground since about 700 MR (the imperial loan layer; the Empire ruled from Sumendar's dwarven mountains, and its capital ruin lies "somewhere in Sumendar's outer reaches", unplaced); Sumendar's General of the Nine is spent (the Ash-Binder); Komo's domain.
- **Map:** about 132,000 sq mi of grassland plain. North: the Order of Steam's mountain wall, a hill-and-forest belt at its foot. North-east: a short bay coast. East: the Dreaming Cape across the Markaa. South-east: Emarrea's hills. South-west: Galdua Jendea's desert across a great border river. West corner: No Man's Land at the Neck. One large walled city at the centre (unnamed); a river from the northern hills south past it; forests west and south; a cluster of steep green peaks with one tall pale spire on the southern edge by Emarrea. Held-heartland density gives about 2.6 to 5.3 million.
- **Siblings:** built: Order of Steam, Dragon's Reach, Eldara, No Man's Land; stubs: Red Dominion, Burdineyja (Kobolds, the Elden gate), Haraour Eliza (Goblins). Built neighbours: the Dreaming Cape, Emarrea, Rika Tikur, No Man's Land; Galdua Jendea a stub around built Valreka.
- **Flags:** the `CLAUDE.md` "cultural (Tahu Tangata)" example removed (GM; replaced with Atarialda); the Markaa's Icelandic name is Lautara's naming-pass matter; the Sumendar domain page's Kobold and Goblin bullets still carry craft language; the **Dwarf** ancestry entry is not feeling-only (two cultures and their jobs baked in), fixed when a build anchors Dwarves.
- **Badges:** none on the pages this build touches.

## Decisions by phase

**Phase 1, the GM's answers (2026-10-01; spitballing for seeds):**
1. **Flow and pressure:** besides the rail and the forge country, No Man's Land's lawless people press on it; the Neck is where that pressure lands, and highway robbery may happen there.
2. **Contradiction:** the fire that renews the land, built as a great cycle: after a great fire new things grow and the land is more fertile, and this is *the* culture for it.
3. **Sibling:** they are the old people (of Sumendar).
4. **Komo's faces:** Destruction and Passion, in the cycle and in indigenous dances that survived the Empire's stranglehold (it need not be that serious). An idea: **singing weapons** used in a dance during fire rituals.
