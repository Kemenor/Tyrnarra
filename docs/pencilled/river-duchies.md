## Phase 0: settled facts

- **Stub, not a rework.** Dropped to "to be defined" on 2026-09-28 (`58f3b99`). The dropped stub: "the green river valley cutting through the desert as an oasis. A loose confederation of noble houses whose wealth comes from controlling river trade", headwaters in Emarrea, the downstream distribution of Emarrea's sake, orchard goods and illusion exports; page tags *Confederation · Oasis*. Not canon.
- **The seed** (`seed-bank.md`, GM 2026-10-02): the oasis in the desert, the green among the dunes, **lazy rivers**, an oasis the size of a kingdom. **GM ruling:** stay distinct from Hareaveldi's many small coloured oases.
- **Traces in built canon (movable, GM):** Emarrea's downstream trade down Hareaveldi's western edge into the Duchies (`emarrea.md`; `emarrea.html`); Valreka's house Asif faces the Duchies (Duchy trade and marriages, "a valley accent"), the herd rests near them, a Duchies factor and chronicler, "a River Duchies toll-quarrel" (`lioaru.md`; the *Short Notice* player page); Galdua's southern rocks, salt to the Duchies' river, lean-year food from "the Duchies' valley", Shuun at "the Duchies landings", a Duchies envoy (`galdua-jendea.md`); Hareaveldi's lean years (`hareaveldi.md`); the Berber family, "a sister" (`_continent.md`; pencilled Kabyle or Tashelhit sound); the Azarketi on "the islands of the River Duchies" (`lioaru.md`). **GM (2026-10-06):** the tolls and the plural duchies are not constraints; the region is unbuilt, and any of these move with the build.
- **Map:** two shapes, ~73,800 sq mi. **The mainland valley** ~50,300 sq mi (~250 × 350 mi), green between the dune seas; the main river from the north-east (Emarrea's barge river) down the middle, a fan of thinner rivers across the plain to the coast; the eastern edge a river line against Hareaveldi. **The one icon**, a capital, at ~(2750, 7076), where the main river meets the head of a deep sea bay. **A desert island** offshore south, ~23,500 sq mi (~250 × 130 mi), sand-coloured, a strait of ~25–50 mi. Neighbours: Galdua Jendea north and west (its pale band between the Duchies and the Lost Kingdom, ~85 mi, the Rot-Tyrant's legions' range); Hareaveldi east; Emarrea north-east, upstream; the Hafra south. Density: a fertile held valley at 30–40/sq mi gives ~1.5–2 million on the mainland; the island at the wild tier 50,000–230,000.
- **GM (2026-10-06):** **the island is open**: it may be recoloured and reimagined, and the capital could move there; inspiration from real or fantasy isles that seem magical. **No rail line is fixed** until the regions are built (the `lioaru.html` rail sentence is a later mirror, not a conflict).
- **Siblings:** all of Lioaru built (Valreka, Galdua Jendea, the Lost Kingdom, Hareaveldi, Auran); Emarrea built upstream.
- **Sameness to watch:** Sugeiturri's River Houses (noble houses that hold the rivers, as a priesthood); Itsasalda (Lautara's river-trade spine, the Vordsbench); Tahu Tangata (a grain plain already selling to Valreka and Galdua); Greenward (a breadbasket); Hareaveldi's oases; Galdua's watering (water given, never sold).
- **Ancestry fixes:** none; no people anchored here.
- **Badges:** none on the Lioaru or Emarrea pages.
