# Deepening Ideas: Tyrnarra

Story-paced enrichment backlog. Everything in this file is **canon-complete**: the structure is decided, published where it should be, and nothing blocks on it. Each entry is a place a future story, session, or deep-dive can land. Genuinely pending canon questions, blocked builds, reworks, and publishing debts live in [`open-threads.md`](open-threads.md); pending map-label work lives in [`map-todo.md`](map-todo.md).

Entries keep the *Decided / Open / Where* format so settled bits stay settled (never re-litigate a *Decided*). When an idea gets built, delete its entry (git history preserves the resolution context) and update [`site-inventory.md`](site-inventory.md) if HTML shipped.

**Decided.** **Uthra, the Out-Longing** is canon: a belief-formed Minor God of Wanderlust (the open road, the setting-out), male-coded, Elysium-resident but landless, **templed nowhere**; coalesced from Dark-Era safe-passage prayers and reshaped by Adventurer-Era adventurers and mendicants. ### [Merkavar] City interior, named figures, traditions *(rolling)*

Three ancestry-blocs canonised: Vishkanya (clergy + craft-guilds), Halfling (Halfling Railworkers' Association, dominant in autumn assembly under the Order of Steam's reduced-fare freight window), Kitsune (ancient parallel tradition to Emarrea, market-readers and information-brokers). The city is further deepened around the seed *the god goes to market* (Jianna's own city, where the goddess of Commerce is sometimes a customer): the **open hand** gesture, the **smile-touched** folk-belief, the cheater-as-heretic ethic, the traveller / local / by-trade **plazas**, worship-is-commerce, and the **⚿ GM Secret: The Stall Jianna Keeps**. Dedicated page at [`/setting/talan/domains/lautara/merkavar/merkavar.html`](../published/setting/talan/domains/lautara/merkavar/merkavar.html); full canon in [lore/geography/lautara.md](../lore/geography/lautara.md), *Notable Locations → Merkavar*.
**Open.** The page-local TBDs flagged in the *Open in the Chronicle Record* panel, plus the named plazas:
- **City layout and districts.** Freight-yard quarter, temple climb toward Helgafjall, market-squares along the western shore, warehouse rings sketched but not detailed; named quarters / plazas / guildhalls / clergy-precincts canon-pending.
- **Named clergy figures.** Senior administrative ranks, high-priest equivalents who chair the standing committees, cross-ancestry clergy whose careers exemplify the cosmopolitan ideal.
- **Named Halfling Railworkers' Association leadership.** The Association's whip, its chief negotiator, its representative on the assembly floor.

- **Historical clergy-vs-assembly arbitration cases.** The three different supremacy answers of the last century each came from a named arbitration body in a named dispute; dispute names, bodies, and legal reasoning all unwritten.

Pace as stories want them. Rolling work.

### [Legea Empire] Doctrinal case-law against Zuzental
**Decided.** The structural tension is **theocracy vs mortal self-governance**, not law-vs-law. *Forseti does not prescribe how mortals live*: her portfolio is judgment and oath-keeping; in Zuzental at large, mortals make their own laws and Forseti's clergy serve as judges, oath-witnesses, and contract-arbiters, not as authors of doctrine. The Empire is the exception: the Faith's god *does* prescribe daily mortal life, which is what makes the Empire a theocracy in the strict sense. **Antiquity resolved:** the Empire was founded in the **first third of the Dark Era** by a holy knight who fought Corrupted spawn, gathered survivors, and ascended to demigodhood through sustained mortal belief plus the Faith's god's recognition; hereditary demigod theocracy ever since. **Forseti's non-intervention is structural, not a choice**: the Gods' Law operates as physics and forbids the bound thirteen from directly governing mortal populations or waging open war against them, which is exactly what crushing the founding cult would have been. Forseti was a constrained spectator, not a tolerant overseer.
**Open.** **Doctrinal case-law collisions**: exactly how the Faith's god-prescribed daily law differs from a Zuzental-Kingdom's mortal-authored daily law at the level of criminal code, contract, succession, oath. The teachings collide in principle; the practical case-law clashes are not yet written. *(The Empire's open names live in `open-threads.md`, the consolidated Legea Empire thread.)*
**Where.** [lore/geography/zuzental.md](../lore/geography/zuzental.md), *Legea Empire → The Divine Faith*. Tension blocks on [legea-empire.html](../published/setting/talan/domains/zuzental/legea-empire.html) frame the principled disagreement; daily-practice consequences are unwritten.
