# PF2e DC Quick Reference (GM Layer)

Set a DC by the **challenge, not the party.** A check's DC is the level or proficiency the *task* demands; the party's level only decides how likely they are to clear it. Pick the base from the right source, then layer difficulty and rarity adjustments on top.

Sources: AoN [Simple DCs (2628)](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2628), [DCs by Level (2629)](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2629), [Adjusting Difficulty (2630)](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2630).

## How to pick a DC

1. **Knowledge / Recall Knowledge** → the **Simple DC** for the proficiency a person would need to know it. A commoner knows untrained-tier facts; a trained scholar knows trained-tier; a specialist, expert-tier. (Who would know this?)
2. **NPC interaction** (Coerce, Make an Impression, Request, Lie) → the **NPC's level-based DC**, then adjust for how motivated they are to resist.
3. **Hazards, traps, the environment** → the **hazard's own level** on the level table.
4. **A task with a clear level** (a level-3 lock, a level-6 ritual) → that level on the level table.

Then apply **difficulty** (±) and **rarity** (+) adjustments.

## The four degrees

Write every quest check with all four:

- **Critical Success** — beat the DC by 10 or more.
- **Success** — meet or beat the DC.
- **Failure** — miss the DC.
- **Critical Failure** — miss by 10 or more.

A natural 20 shifts the degree one step **up**; a natural 1 shifts it one step **down**.

## Worked examples (*The Narrows Job*)

- **The Jianna/Nirfel statue — Recall Knowledge (Religion), DC 17.** Identifying a specific Vice Demon is trained-scholar knowledge → Simple DC 15 (Trained); the demon is unlabelled, so you are inferring it → Hard **+2** → **17**. Critical Success adds the GM-tier irony; Failure gives only the surface read.
- **Prying the cargo out of Edrik — Diplomacy, DC 23.** Anchor on Edrik, a level-3 NPC → DC 18; he is under standing orders to say nothing → Very Hard **+5** → **23**. Critical Failure (realistically a fumble) sees him move to cancel the contract.
