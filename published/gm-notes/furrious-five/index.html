What This Is

These pages hold the table-facing detail that does not belong on the public site: read-aloud boxes, stat blocks, the floors below the floors, NPC secrets, adventure hooks, and the active plot threads. The player-facing Millhaven page and the Wayward Compass page carry only the chronicler-tier and folk-tier canon; the GM-tier mechanisms and the live campaign live here.

This layer is deliberately kept out of the player sidebar. Treat every page under /gm-notes/ as behind the screen.
