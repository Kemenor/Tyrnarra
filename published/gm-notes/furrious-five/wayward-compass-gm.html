The Building

The Wayward Compass has occupied its spot on the central bridge since 1,891 MR, over six hundred years, through the tail end of the Dark Era and everything after. It did not start as a guildhall. It started as a storage shed that a group of adventurers rented by the week, then could not stop renting because it was convenient, and then quietly bought when the owner died without heirs.

It has grown the way the bridge district has grown: upward first, then outward, then eventually downward onto pilings that were originally just meant to support a practice post for Kael's sparring. The original shed is now somewhere inside the walls of the second floor. Nobody is entirely sure which walls. Rumour has it the shed still houses an artifact of the original trio who founded the hall. Aldric will scowl if you go looking for it.

From the bridge level it presents a respectable face: a wide door, the compass-rose sign, Mira Seld's desk visible through the front window. From the river it looks like something that decided it wanted to be a boat and could not commit, ropes running between the piling extensions and the bridge above. The sound of the river is constant, louder in the lower levels, a soft undertone in the upper floors. Regulars stop noticing it. New arrivals find it either calming or unsettling.

What Makes It Feel Like Home
Maps cover almost every wall: some current, some historical, some of places nobody has been able to identify for decades. Trophy cases line the drinking-hall stairs: a basilisk scale, a piece of what might be Elden stonework, three weapons of unusual make with small plaques listing the names of the people who brought them back. The plaques list when they were recovered. They do not always list what happened to the person who recovered them. Regulars know which ones to avoid reading.

Mira Seld
Branch Receptionist · Vishkanya, mid-30s
Tap ▾
The first face every adventurer sees, and the last one they want to disappoint. Mira runs the front desk with terrifying competence and a slit-pupil smile that never quite reaches her eyes.
Personality
Unflappable. Warm but watchful. She remembers every name, every face, every outstanding fee, and keeps the branch's contract-memory the way a Reckoner keeps a Watchroll. Speaks to hardened veterans the same way she speaks to terrified first-timers: calmly, clearly, with exactly the right amount of expectation.
Physical
A Vishkanya of Itsasalda stock: fine scales beaded along the hairline, the backs of the hands, and the line of the throat, catching the lamplight when she turns; vertical-slit pupils that fix on you a half-second before she speaks. Practical dark hair pinned back, ink-stained fingers, three quills tucked behind one ear. She moves with the still economy of someone who has decided exactly how much motion a thing needs and will not spend more.
Clothing & Dress
The guild uniform, navy and gold, worn with the precision of someone who ironed it this morning and will iron it again tomorrow: collar high, badge pinned exactly level, sleeves buttoned to the wrist over the scaled forearms, kept neat rather than hidden. One concession to vanity, a fine scrivener's chain at the wrist.
Notable Traits
  • Knows every active contract, who took it, and when it is overdue
  • Can de-escalate a barfight using only tone of voice
  • Sends handwritten follow-up notes after dangerous missions
  • Fiercely protective of new recruits; quietly tests veterans
Guild Stats
Perception +8Insight +9Persuasion +7Rank 7 (Retired Field)
⚿ GM Secret
Mira is a former field agent, Rank 7 clearance, who burned out after a classified operation went wrong. The Head Office offered a desk as a mercy posting. She took it, and discovered she is better at this than she ever was at fieldwork. She could go back, and the old field discipline is still in her blood, Vishkanya-literal. She will not.

Velka Dorn
Quartermaster & Archivist · Shisk (lapsed cleric of Enki), 40s
Tap ▾
Manages the equipment locker, the contract archive, and the deep records room, the last of which most people do not know exists. Was once one of Enki's Sealed; now serves a different kind of truth.
Personality
Meticulous, precise, with flashes of dark humour that appear without warning. Has opinions about how things are stored, filed, and returned. Will lend equipment to a party she trusts; will not lend her good ink to anyone.
Physical
A Shisk, tall and angular: pebbled grey-green hide and the quill-ridge down the back of the skull gone prematurely pale, silver where it should be dark. Reading-lenses she refuses to admit she needs, perched where her brow-spines begin. She carries a ring of keys too large for the building's known doors and moves through her own domain without once looking for anything.
Clothing & Dress
Simple grey robes, guild badge pinned exactly level, and the high covered collar of Enki's Sealed she never quite gave up wearing. Ink dried under the claws. Nothing about her dress would tell you she once wore a church's seal, which is the point.
Notable Traits
  • Knows where every piece of guild equipment is, including the pieces that are not logged
  • Has read every contract filed at this branch going back 30 years
  • Can authenticate a document in seconds and spot a forgery in less, the Sealed's old training
  • Does not gossip. Does tell you what you need to know, sometimes unprompted.
Guild Stats
History +10Investigation +9Religion +7 (Enki)Arcana +6Former Rank 5 (Church Transfer)
⚿ GM Secret
The deep records room holds files on guild operations that predate the current Charter. Velka has read them all. Some describe things that, technically, the guild should not have survived doing. She catalogued them, said nothing, and started keeping her own coded notes. "Shisk-curiosity" is the warning Enki's novices are given about her kind; she is the reason it is a warning. She serves the guild. She also serves what the guild claims to stand for, and she is watching to see if those stay the same thing.

Kael Orvaine
Field Trainer & Combat Coordinator · orc, late 30s
Tap ▾
Runs orientation for new recruits, posts the difficulty ratings on contracts, and is responsible for the sign on the wall that reads "DEAD MEMBERS COMPLETE NO QUESTS." A pragmatist who has made peace with caring.
Personality
Measured, economical with words, will give you exactly the advice you need to not die, once. Does not repeat himself, the way his people teach the hunt: clearly, a single time, and you live. Has a laugh that is rare and worth earning. Holds a deep professional respect for competence wherever he finds it.
Physical
An orc out of the Hunt lands, built like a door and moving like something that has been hurt before and decided to learn from it. Grey-green hide, a heavy brow, one chipped lower tusk; he says it is from a training accident and everyone assumes otherwise. Always in lightweight scale mail, even indoors.
Clothing & Dress
Lightweight scale over a trainer's worn leathers, worn indoors as readily as out; the guild badge riveted to a shoulder strap rather than pinned, because pins fail. Nothing decorative. Everything on him is rated for a fall.
Notable Traits
  • Rates every incoming party within 10 minutes of watching them interact
  • Keeps informal notes on which contract types suit which party compositions
  • Will pull a contract for a group he thinks is not ready yet, and explain exactly why
  • Runs sparring sessions at dawn that anyone can join, no questions asked
Guild Stats
Athletics +9Perception +7Insight +6Survival +7Tactics (Expert)Former Rank 6
⚿ GM Secret
Kael lost a full party on a Head Office sanctioned mission he was told was low-risk. The official report listed it as a field accident. He has the real orders. He kept the job because walking away felt like abandoning the people the guild could still protect. He does not know what he will do if he discovers Aldric knew the mission was compromised.

Dova Crestmark
Guildhall Barkeeper · Itsasaldan kitsune, appears ageless
Tap ▾
Runs the guildhall's tavern with iron authority and an endless supply of something she calls "the good stuff." Nobody knows her age, her hometown, or how she got the job. Nobody asks twice.
Personality
Blunt, generous in unexpected moments, and possessed of an unsettling ability to know what you need before you say it, the way an Itsasalda bar-owner holds a harbour's worth of secrets and pours instead of telling. Has a philosophy about drinking that sounds like tavern wisdom until you realise it is actually life advice.
Physical
An anthro fox of the Itsasalda bar-owner line: russet-and-cream fur gone silver-frosted at the muzzle and the ear-tips, a tail-count of years she declines to discuss. Compact and broad across the shoulders, low to her own bar. Warm dark eyes that miss nothing. Burn scars on both forearms she does not explain, the fur grown back pale over them. She laughs like she means it.
Clothing & Dress
An apron with so many pockets it is practically armour, hung with bar-cloths and bottle-keys; nothing flashy, nothing that draws a second look. The Itsasalda bar-owner's plainness is deliberate: she holds information, she does not advertise it.
Notable Traits
  • Never forgets a drink order or a secret told at her bar
  • Has a complex unofficial credit system that somehow works
  • Can read a room's tension level in seconds and calibrate accordingly
  • Lights the back-bar lamp without a taper; the "good stuff" is foxfire-warmed
  • Makes a non-alcoholic house brew she gives to people she is worried about
Guild Stats
Perception +8Sleight of Hand +6Brewing (Legendary)Guild Informant
⚿ GM Secret
Dova is technically a guild employee but also a named node in the Itsasalda bar-owner network, that parallel, not hostile, power that holds the coast's information and answers to no council. She passes on nothing that harms her regulars. She has, once, passed on something that saved the whole branch without anyone knowing it came from her. The arrangement is old, the details classified even from Aldric. She and Sable Rei are kitsune of traditions so long diverged they recognise only the foxfire in each other, two information-powers sharing one town, and neither has ever said so aloud.

Pip Sallow
Guildhall Server & Odd-Jobs · halfling, early 20s · Millhaven foundling
Tap ▾
Fastest-moving thing in the guildhall, somehow always where he is needed and almost never where he is not. Enthusiastic to a fault, desperately wants to be an adventurer, and is being gently protected from that ambition by everyone who likes him.
Personality
Bright-eyed, relentlessly cheerful, asks too many questions about what it is like "out there." Has a talent for being invisible when useful and visible when annoying. Genuinely kind. More perceptive than he looks.
Physical
Small even for a halfling, with unruly brown curls and ink smudges on his wrists from writing down orders he forgets to stop taking. The local boy of the staff, Millhaven-bred and bridge-quick on his feet. Always slightly out of breath.
Clothing & Dress
The tavern apron two sizes too big, sleeves rolled and re-rolled, hand-me-down boots, and a half-finished map-roll forever poking out of a back pocket. Dressed like someone who is technically staff and quietly rehearsing for a different job.
Notable Traits
  • Has memorised everyone's usual order
  • Draws maps of "the adventures he's going to have someday," some disturbingly accurate
  • Has a nose for when people are lying, just does not know what to do with it yet
  • The guildhall cats follow him. All of them.
Guild Stats
Stealth +5Perception +4Persuasion +4Sleight of Hand +3
⚿ GM Secret
Pip is the illegitimate child of a guild Rank 9 operative who does not know he exists. Pip does not know who his parent is. He just knows the guild took him in here in Millhaven when he was seven and no one has ever told him the full story. He stopped asking. He has not stopped wondering. One of his "adventure maps" accidentally sketched a location that his missing parent's last mission was centred on.
