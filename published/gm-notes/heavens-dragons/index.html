Session 0

Session 0 covers both campaigns: this one and the Available on Short Notice one-shots.

The goal. Rebellion against the Elden.

Shape. A loud campaign. Sandbox, advisor-guided: roughly location to location, with total freedom inside each location. Smaller sandboxes queued together, with more narrative focus once things start moving.

Tone. A more serious campaign. Silliness is fine; seriousness in the right moments.

House rule: skill sharing in social scenes. If the party agrees, a roll can use the highest modifier among the party members present.

Lines and veils
