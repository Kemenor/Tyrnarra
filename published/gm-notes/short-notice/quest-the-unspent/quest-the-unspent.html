The Premise

The party is crossing the Midarra as hired guards on the stillship Goodmeasure, carrying luxury cargo north to Solkai: a Guild job taken on short notice in Rika Tikur. The master trusts no Lord's colours and bought none; he hired them instead. Poachers attack; Ninehulls' fleet blasts the poachers apart, and the Pirate Lord apologises, carries the party to Freeboard, and keeps their ship for a deck. Skysail comes home for the Mooring. Merrin out of the Jigger, captain of the broke crew that keeps Mara Goldwake's flagship the Unspent, hires the stranded Guild party to open it.

Inside, behind the dead crew who kept her shut, Goldwake sits at her table with an Elden lift-heart in her hand. The first air in 140 years makes her arm fall; the heart wakes and drags the islet the Jigger down toward Freeboard. The party carries it up through the Jigger's Elden holds and seats it in the engine. Then Ninehulls comes for Goldwake's hoard, and the party learns what it means to kill a man who cannot die.

The Table

Party level 11. Encounters built for 5 combatants (low 75 · moderate 100 · severe 150 · extreme 200 XP); every scene works for all 6 at the table; play goes on with players missing. House rule from Session 0: a social roll may use the highest modifier among the party members present.

Running Threads

The telling. Pell and the tellers tell the party's deeds from the moment of the capture. By the end the party is told bigger than Ninehulls, which is why he comes; the Last Hands give them a told name from the deed the room loved most.

"Yours by right." Nirfel's line runs through the whole quest: the keepers' vote, the cabin, the night whisper, the share-out. Whoever takes from Goldwake's cache carries the Greed-mark.

What the party leaves rigged. The kill-gangway, the swivel guns and the ship's tilt from the first boarding are the arena for the fight with Ninehulls.
