Ninehulls' Crew

The Pirate Lord who takes the Goodmeasure, the quartermaster who sails her home, and the mate who opens the barrel.

Ninehulls
Pirate Lord of the sea · the man who cannot die · Full
Tap ▾
Took nine ships in one night. A wide smile, real manners, and a fleet behind him. Apologises to the party for the poachers and takes their ship anyway.
Pronouns
he/him
Ancestry · age
Human · about 50
Occupation
A Pirate Lord of the sea
Level
Built with the final fight: a level below the boss budget, so The Man Who Cannot Die can carry his second life
Size
Two metres thirty, built like a wardrobe: a Large token. He grows a hair with every telling, a running joke on the Midarra that is true.
Looks
Square in every direction: shoulders a doorframe wide, hands that make a tankard look like a thimble, deep sun-brown skin. Scalp shaved clean; a thick black beard in heavy curled plaits bound with brass rings. A pale, puckered scar runs across his throat and down his chest, from the wound that should have killed him and started the legend; he leaves his shirt open to show it. Eyes that crinkle; a wide smile with every tooth still in it.
Dress
A long black captain's coat, so much cloth that the tellers say it was cut from the mainsail of the first of the nine, lined in the red of his flag and patched wherever blades have found him, a red sash wound at the waist. No hat: nothing fits. Bare forearms, heavy boots, and a cutlass forged to his own scale, an ordinary sword in his hand and a two-hander in anyone else's.
Voice
Warm and booming, never in a hurry, courteous to a fault. Apologises sincerely and takes your ship anyway. Laughs at himself before anyone else can.
Sample lines
"Now what are you doing in my waters?" · "I won't set you adrift in the jolly boat. I'm not an animal. But the ship's mine."
Mannerisms
Claps once when he has decided something. Ducks every beam in Freeboard with the ease of a man who has hit every one of them once.
Disposition
Friendly and amused toward the party, until the tellers start telling them bigger than him
Goal
To be told. Every raid, every apology and every round he pays for is a story.
Faction & ties
His fleet and crews · Halfpenny, his quartermaster
Status
Alive; dies to the party at the end, and walks into their tavern three nights later

The man died in an ambush in Freeboard. The Midarra believed he could not die, and the belief made a being shaped as him, which knows only the story and has no given name. It struck down the man the taverns blamed, who was the wrong man. It lives on being told; a forgotten Lord stops being. Canon: lore/geography/askamira/twin-cities.md, ⚿ Ninehulls.

Appears: Struck Colours · the broadside · The Last Hands · the fight · the round

Quartermaster of Ninehulls' flagship; captain of the Goodmeasure for the run to Freeboard
Level
Level 11 · Non-Combat Level 11 (the books, the shares) · full stat block with the Ninehulls fight
Looks
Tall and spare, with silver-grey hair cropped short against the wind, a long patient face and ink worked so deep into her fingertips it will never wash out. A worn brass half-penny hangs on a cord at her throat: the coin the tellers say she split a prize down to.
Dress
A long sea-coat with more pockets than seams, a tally-stick in every one; sleeves rolled to the elbow; a wax tablet and stylus at her hip where another officer would wear a pistol. Good boots, badly kept.
Voice
A quartermaster's dry arithmetic: numbers as punctuation, never a wasted word, never raised.
Sample line
"You're worth eleven shares on that ship and three on this one. Don't make me count you twice."
Mannerisms
Taps the half-penny when she is deciding; writes before she answers; looks at hands, not faces, during a vote.
Disposition
Indifferent; Friendly to anyone who takes a fair share and no more
Goal
Every share even, every hand paid, the books true. Ninehulls' crew stay his because she makes it worth it.
Background
Earned her name splitting a prize before the hands down to the half-penny. Has sailed with Ninehulls since before the legend. Calls the Last Hands over him at the end.
Faith

Faction & ties

Alive
