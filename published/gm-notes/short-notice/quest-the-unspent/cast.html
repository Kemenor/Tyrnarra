Pirate Lord of the sea · the man who cannot die
☠️ Ninehulls

Took nine ships in one night. A wide smile, real manners, and a fleet behind him. Apologises to the party for the poachers and takes their ship anyway.

Notes
Voice: warm, amused, generous with drink and never with a prize. He keeps to the articles. Stats: to build (the final boss, a level lower to carry the second life).

The man died in an ambush in Freeboard. The Midarra believed he could not die, and the belief made a being shaped as him, which knows only the story and has no given name. It struck down the man the taverns blamed, who was the wrong man. It lives on being told; a forgotten Lord stops being. Canon: lore/geography/askamira/twin-cities.md, ⚿ Ninehulls.

Appears Struck Colours · the broadside · The Last Hands · the fight · the round
Ninehulls' quartermaster · a told name
🪙 Halfpenny

Told for splitting a prize before the hands down to the half-penny, every share even. Counts everything, including you.

Notes
Voice: a quartermaster's dry arithmetic. Calls the Last Hands over Ninehulls. Stats: to build.
Appears Struck Colours · The Last Hands
Poacher captain · a bought name
🚩 Redsail

An unflagged captain who paid the tellers before he had the deed. His crew know it.

Notes
Dies to Ninehulls at the opening; his crew vote deposition and sign under the black flag. Stats: to build with his crew.
Appears the opening
Master of the Goodmeasure · Kholo
⚓ Master Ozhra

A Kholo of a ferry-clan of the Solkai crossing, keeping his own. Skimped on colours and knows it the moment the red sail shows.

Notes
A placeholder name until Tvisol has a personal-name register. Non-combatant.
Appears Struck Colours
A teller of the Lower Spar
📣 Pell out of the Spar

Tells the Lords' deeds for coin, and tells the party's before they have finished doing them.

Notes
Voice: a working storyteller's patter; talks about legends as stock to be kept, and about money plainly. "Anyone can buy a telling. Nobody can buy the second one."
Appears Freeboard · the Last Hands
Lord of Freeboard · cameo
⚖️ Odile Brasshand

A quartermaster who rose to the seat. Wants every Lord to honour every Lord's colours.

Notes
Voice: dry, numerate, unsentimental about Lords. Optional at the Mooring.
Appears the Mooring
Lord of Skysail and a Pirate Lord · Tengu
🪶 Kesti Downdraft

Brisk and practical; hands a visitor the useful thing first. Commands outright while the Jigger falls.

Notes
Starts the sink clock, lends Hobb, can have the Main take lines for a favour. Pays the party coin and passage afterwards.
Appears the briefing · The Last Hands
Captain of the Unspent's keepers
🗝️ Merrin out of the Jigger

Voted in on the promise to open the Unspent and sell what is inside. Hungry, proud, and the one who opens the door if the party hesitates.

Notes
The keepers are the grandchildren of Goldwake's other two crews, and poor. Nirfel's whisper ("yours by right") reached them first.
Appears the hire · the share-out
A tender of the old work
🕯️ Old Hobb out of the Jigger

Knows every rite of the Jigger by rote and none of the reasons. His humming is the Wake step.

Notes
Non-combatant guide on the inside route; cannot make the outside climb.
Appears the tended holds
The dead Lord · now Nirfel
🪶 Mara Goldwake

Preserved at her table in the airless cabin, the lift-heart in her closed hand.

Notes
Her soul is in Bolverk as Nirfel, the Greed Vice Demon; her body is only a body. Canon: lore/geography/bolverk.md, Nirfel.
Appears first air
The whisper · Greed
💰 Nirfel

"It is not anyone else's. It is yours by right." Whispers to the keepers, then the party.

Notes
She acts only by whisper. Taking from her cache leaves the Greed-mark; the Guild knows a ritual to burn it off.
Appears Struck Colours · the night whisper
