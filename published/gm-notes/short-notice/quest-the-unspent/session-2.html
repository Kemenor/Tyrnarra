The Sink Clock · the session's real enemy

8 stages. +1 per 10-minute activity (Treat Wounds, Refocus, Repair, Identify), per critical failure at a hazard or the puzzle, and for the gallery. −1 at the hanging fleet, through Kesti's lines (costs a favour), or by dumping weight (homes into the Midarra).

1 an airship tears free · 2 the Jigger tilts, gardens slide off · 3 the Main lurches · 4 the dead Lord's spire falls · 5 debris on the top decks · 6 the Jigger settles on the stack · 7 the stack buckles · 8 the engine stops; the Jigger falls and takes part of the lower city.

1–5 are warnings with a cost; 6–7 grim but fixable (casualties, a bleak telling, Kesti's pay shrinks); 8 is true failure. Played clean, there is room for about two rests.

The Carry

The heart has to go up to the Jigger's empty socket. Near it, lift fails: no flying, and loose things drift toward it. The engine's defenders go for whoever carries it.

1 · The Fall
Merrin out of the Jigger
Read Aloud · draft

The Unspent drops a full length on her lines and every rope screams. Loose gear slides past you toward the heart, which rolls on the deck as though the deck ran downhill in every direction.

Above, the keepers are hauling baskets up hand over hand. They are hauling up the chests.

What happens

A skill challenge: grab the heart and get off the sagging flagship. The keepers save the hoard before they help anyone: Nirfel's greed, shown and not named.

2 · Kesti's Briefing
Kesti DowndraftOld Hobb
Read Aloud · draft

Kesti Downdraft lands on the Unspent's rail from a skiff that is still moving and gives you the useful thing first. "That thing is pulling my islet down. There's a hollow in the old work that's always been empty; the tenders' rites go round it. Get it there. You have until she touches the spires."

Behind her, an old man in a tender's apron is already climbing over the rail. "Hobb," she says. "He knows the way in. Don't let him fall."

What happens

The Lord commands outright while the city is under attack or under way. She starts the sink clock and offers Hobb as a non-combatant guide. Asking her to have the Main take more lines buys a stage back, at the cost of a favour.

3 · The Underside
Old Hobb
Read Aloud · draft

Under the Jigger the world turns over: rock overhead, roots hanging in a beard on every side, airships straining on their lines, and a long way below, the Midarra and the stacked city waiting for whatever falls.

Old Hobb points a crooked finger at a hatch in the rock. "The tended way's in there." Then, as if it does not matter: "Wind goes in somewhere up yonder too. Always has."

What happens

Inside, the reading: slower (the gallery costs a stage), and the party arrives at the boss knowing all four seating steps. Outside, open air: faster, saves lives at the hanging fleet, and the party arrives knowing only Cradle. The outside way has to be found: Hobb's hint is the clue, and the Gate is its first test.

Exploration · inside route
A1 · The Tended Holds
Old Hobb
Read Aloud · draft

Inside, the rock gives way to something older: smooth grey walls that curve like a ship's ribs, lit by lamps the tenders have hung for generations.

Hobb walks it like his own kitchen. At each brass plate he stops, touches it three times, hums four notes, and moves on. He has done it every day of his life and has no idea why.

What happens

Hobb's rite by rote is the Wake step of the seating. A player who copies or records it knows Wake at the boss. Hobb cannot follow the outside climb; inside, he comes as far as the gear chamber.

A3 + A4 · Wardens and Gallery
The wardens
Read Aloud · draft

The passage opens into halls of pale stone and bronze, too regular to be cave and too old to be anything the pirates built. Something ahead clicks, then clicks again in answer.

At the top of a long stair a great round vault waits: an orrery of brass rings turning slowly around nothing, its walls covered in writing that seems to slide when you look at it.

What happens

The wardens: Elden constructs woken by the engine's distress; they rush the carrier through the halls and gates. Moderate. The gallery: the Silent Tongue on the vault walls; reading it costs a stage and reveals Turn and Bind. The upper gate can be locked to mark its DC. The side passage behind debris is a place for optional treasure: clearing it costs time.

Keep the Corrupted God dial low: a critical failure on the reading brings a moment of dread from the writing, nothing more.

B1 · The Gate
Old Hobb
Read Aloud · draft

Hobb was right. Under the lip of the rock, a little to the east, the air is moving: the clouds bend toward a dark slot in the stone and vanish into it.

Between you and it there is nothing at all: thirty feet of open sky, and the heart in your arms pulling at every lift in the air around it.

What happens

Spot the shaft mouth (Perception or Survival, reading the draught), then cross open air with no lift near the heart (Athletics or Acrobatics). Any good plan can replace a check: Skysail skiffs dropping lines, Kesti's crews swinging them across, carrying the heart apart from the flyers. Failure: +1 stage, and the party goes in the inside way.

Hazard room · both routes
A5 / B4 · The Gear Chamber
Old Hobb
Read Aloud · draft

The shaft ends in a round chamber the size of a temple, and all of it is moving: walkways spiralling in toward a great turning heart of brass, gears the size of houses grinding at the edges, cables swinging loose overhead.

Everything leans toward the centre. The moment you step out of the stairwell you feel it lean on you, too.

The room's rules

Goal: the carrier and the heart reach the exit (top stairs). Inside route enters at the lower-right stairs, outside at the bottom stairs. Foundry: 140 px grid, 5 ft, 33 × 46 squares, no walls.

The pull: drift toward the governor, light on the outer walkways, strong on the inner ones, more for the carrier. Two Strides + Brace means no check; three Strides means a pull check at the end of the turn. The carrier's Brace needs Athletics (an ally can Aid). Off a walkway is a fall down the shaft: +1 stage, back in at the bottom stairs.

The clockworks: G5 and G6 dangerous, G8 easy, always turning until stopped at the governor. Passing one: critical success no damage, success half, failure full damage and blocked. A dangerous clockwork's critical failure throws the creature off: one chance to grab a ledge, or an adjacent ally catches them and takes half the damage.

The strikes: four sections each round on a three-round cycle, never two neighbours together; plus one wild strike. Reading the pattern (Perception or Crafting after a round of watching) lets the GM mark next round's four sections from then on; spotting the wild strike costs an action each round. The cycle, the d12 table and the ten pre-rolled wild strikes are in the area notes and in the strike chart.

The governor: reached by the jumps (all but impossible for the carrier). Crafting, Arcana or Thievery stops a clockwork for 1 round, 2 on a critical success. The crumbling ground gives way under a Medium creature.

Still to build
  • Hazard stats: clockwork damage and DCs, strike damage and Reflex DC, pull DCs, the reading DC.
  • The Foundry macro: the sections as drawings, next round's strikes shown by click.

Boss
4 · The Engine Heart
The engine's warden
Read Aloud · draft

Heat hits you like a hand. The engine of the Jigger fills the chamber from floor to vault: rivers of fire on either side of a narrow walkway, belts grinding over the flames, and at the top, behind a great still wheel, a dark round socket the size of a shield.

Between you and it, something unfolds from the machinery and turns to face you.

The fight

Three ways to the socket: the centre stair from the lower wheel (short, through the warden), the side stairs to the gallery and its gangways, or the side gantries round the warden's platform entirely. The gangways spawn smaller defenders as the engine hazard runs.

Seating the heart, four steps in order at the socket, each 2 actions + a check: Cradle (Athletics, always known) · Turn (Crafting or Arcana) · Wake (Occultism, Religion or Performance) · Bind (Thievery or Crafting). A known step is a standard DC; an unknown one needs a Recall Knowledge action first and a harder DC. Critical failure: the engine lashes the seater. Critical success: the step is done and the engine holds still a round. Inside arrives with all four known; outside with Cradle only.

The fire is a hazard placed on the lava; the belts carry whoever stands on them. Use the Silver variant while the engine is cold, the Rising variants as the hazard worsens, the Original once the heart is seated.

Still to build
  • Encounter: the warden (severe) + the engine as a complex hazard + gangway spawns, near extreme in total.

Resolution
5 · The Rise
Kesti Downdraft
Read Aloud · draft

The heart clicks home. For a moment nothing happens. Then the engine breathes in, and warmth rolls out of the old work in a wave you feel through your boots.

The islet stops falling. It rises, slowly, lines slackening all around it, and from somewhere far above comes a sound you did not expect to hear in here: the whole of Skysail cheering.

What happens

The Jigger warms for the first time anyone remembers, and its airships have less to carry. Cloud-water rains on Freeboard again, as relief. Where the sink clock stopped decides how the tellers tell it.
