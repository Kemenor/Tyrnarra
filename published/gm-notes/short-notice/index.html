The lore is in lore/geography/lioaru.md under Valreka; correct it there first and this layer follows.

What This Is

A one-shot campaign, so the shape is different from the Furrious Five's. There is no home town to dossier and no district map to walk. What the party has instead is a standing: they are on the Roll, the Whalehall knows where to find them, and each session opens with a rider arriving or a posting already in hand.

That buys two things worth having at a one-shot table. Any premise anywhere on Talan is one line of setup away, because the Guild ledger already reaches there. And a rotating cast is free: whoever turned up this week is whoever the desk raised this week, and the ones who did not are out on another contract.

The city is the constant, not the map. Valreka is fully built on the player-facing worldbuilding page; run the returns-to-town out of that, and keep the GM-tier of it (Tani riding with the Strays as Amona) where it belongs.

The one secret already on the board
Tani walks her own city in three faces and the city knows two of them. The third rides Stokkul with the mother whale's Strays under the name Amona, and the crew will go to their graves not knowing. Canon in lore/geography/lioaru.md, Valreka → ⚿ GM Secret: Amona of the Strays. A party on the Roll lives on the whales, which means they can meet her; nothing about the dispatch premise needs to touch it until you want it to.
