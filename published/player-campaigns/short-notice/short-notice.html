Where You Stand

You are Adventurers' Guild hands who take work on short notice: a crew that keeps its gear packed and its name current with the desk, and goes where a posting will not keep. Right now you are in Merkavar, where you took your latest Guild posting.

Merkavar is Jianna's god-city, the Market City, built on a great inland lake at the foot of Helgafjall, the sacred peak that rises from the water's centre. The snow on its summit never fully melts, and the lake at its foot has never frozen. Market-plazas ring the shore, and the quick way between any two is across the water, so ferries, barges and boat-vendors work the lake all day and deals are struck hull-to-hull over the gunwale. A warehouse-floor at peak hour holds half the continent in one room. Shorting a customer their weight is nearer to blasphemy than to crime here, since the stranger at the scale might be the goddess.

Your work comes through Merkavar's Godshall, the Adventurers' Guild's seat in the Market City and one of the thirteen great halls the Guild keeps in the god-cities.

Reading These Pages

Everything here is yours to read. It holds what your characters would know as Guild hands on short notice: the city you are standing in, how the work finds you, and the terms a posting is offered on. What is actually waiting at the far end of a contract stays where it belongs, which is at the table.

How the Work Reaches You

Most crews take work off a board. Yours keeps its name on a list instead: the desk's register of parties who have declared themselves available on short notice. An entry gives your names, your rank, what you will take, and where a runner can find you. You get on with your life, and when something lands that will not keep, the desk sends for you. A crew that keeps its entry current can be raised, briefed, and on the road inside a day.

The clerks have read that phrase off the register so many times that it is simply what such a party is called. It is where your name came from, and the joke is entirely at the Guild's expense.

The pay travels as easily as you do. The Guild Bank's enchanted ledgers keep every office's balances in step, so a fee paid in at one Guild hall can be drawn at any other on Talan.

The upshot is that a short-notice crew guards a dig one week and walks into a toll-quarrel the next, and sometimes boards a ship for somewhere it has never heard of because the posting said it would not keep. The desk sends whoever is ready ahead of whoever is near.
