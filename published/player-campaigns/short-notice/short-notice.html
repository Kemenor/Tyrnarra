Where You Stand

You are based in Valreka, Tani's god-city, which is carried across the dune-sea of Galdua Jendea on the backs of titanic sand-whales and is never in the same place two seasons running. Each noble house guides one whale and lives on its back; the crown is whoever guides the mother whale, oldest of the herd, who has carried the city since the goddess came back. When the herd draws close the contraption-bridges go up and the whole city is one place. When it spreads to travel, every whale is its own island, and you are home or you are a guest somewhere else for two days.

Your work comes from the Whalehall, the Adventurers' Guild's Godshall in Valreka and the only one of the thirteen that travels. The Guild guides no whale and is no house, so it can hold no district of its own; it rides the mother whale at the crown's leave, in the same old quarter as the memory-keepers, the city's two institutions that own no beast. Say Whalehall on the decks and Valreka's Godshall on the paperwork; both are correct and both get used daily.

How the Work Reaches You

A settled Guild board fills from the street. Valreka's fills in bursts. When the herd draws near enough to a settlement, a season's worth of postings comes aboard at once; then the city crosses empty dune for weeks and the wall goes quiet. Waiting at the hall for something to appear is a way to waste a month.

So the desk keeps a register instead: the Standing Roll, the list of parties who have declared themselves available on short notice. An entry gives your names, your rank, what you will take, and where on the herd an outrider can find you. You do not sit in the hall. You are out on the whales living your life, and when something lands that will not keep, a rider comes across the sand for you. A crew that keeps its entry current can be raised, briefed, and put on the sand inside a day.

The clerks have read that phrase off the register so many times that it is simply what such a party is called. It is where your name came from, and the joke is entirely at the Guild's expense.

The work itself comes from everywhere. The Guild Bank's enchanted ledgers do not notice the whales at all, so a contract posted at a Kingshall four domains away reaches Valreka's counter the same day it reaches anyone's. The Guild Post is the slower half: a letter addressed to a roaming city goes to wherever the herd happens to be, which is a thing nobody outside Galdua Jendea knows, so the hall pays the Outrider Corps to run its sacks out to the River Duchies' edge and the scattered desert settlements and meet the settled routes there.

The upshot is that a Roll party guards a dig in the dune-sea one week and walks into a River Duchies toll-quarrel the next, and sometimes boards a ship for somewhere it has never heard of because the posting said it would not keep. The Whalehall sends whoever is ready ahead of whoever is near. It is the one Godshall on Talan where distance to the work is the second question the desk asks.
