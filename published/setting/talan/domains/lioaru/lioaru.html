Lioaru

A land where time doesn't move cleanly. Things don't fully pass.

At a Glance

Etymology
Icelandic líð (passage of time, slipping away) → drifted to Lioaru
Position
Southwest Talan, south of Sumendar, west of Ezkudon, south-west of Lautara, coastal on Hafra to the south and west
Terrain
Desert dominant with pale sand dunes throughout. A green river valley cuts through as an oasis of life. The dark, shadowy Lost Kingdom occupies the south-west coast. Lost Isle sits offshore. Temporal anomalies throughout: moments that repeat, ruins newer than they should be.
Character
Desert, temporal anomalies, ancient ruins
Temporal MagicUnstable TimeAncient Ruins
Bound God
Tani: domain of Time
Peoples
Three dominant ancestries are anchored here, standing in the three tenses of the goddess: Ghoran (the ones who hold what was; the Guardians of Time), Azarketi (the ones who answer it now; Valreka's water-bearers), and Nagaji (the ones who become what's next; shed, and whole again — heartland Hareaveldi, the serpent-kingdom of the desert).
Magitrain
Lines reach the River Duchies and Hareaveldi from the wider southern network; the desert interior is served by caravan, the mobile capital Valreka is served by nothing scheduled, and the Lost Kingdom has no rail and never will: the Blackened Lands' chaos-magic scrambles Arcanotech precision, and no consortium would build there anyway.

Sub-Regions

Galdua Jendea · The Rocks of the Dune Sea →
The Court Tongue: the lost people; the older chronicles read the Place of Many Waters
The great dune sea at Lioaru's heart, the sand-whales' range and Valreka's home ground, and Azrar, the Necklace: nineteen rock massifs standing out of the sand, each a night's sandsail run from the next. Seventeen live, their cities hung on the shaded faces of the rock and fed by the springs inside it; two are lost. The Galduans are the people who stayed on the rock when the faithful took to the whales, and the springs water the herd at every watering, given and never sold. Yemmazru, the great rock, holds its eight daughter rocks by the debt, beside eight unfallen rocks, under the sunrise law. Sandsails cross the sand by night with the salt; wings ride the noon. The Ghoran's fullest home on Talan, and the only source of tagnit, the sand that keeps things from aging.

Lost Kingdom · The Blackened Lands →
Plain Talanese; folk name the black spot on the map
The south-west coast where Tani was struck down: black ground under a standing haze, time-warped strata, ruins that read newer than they should, the dead rising unbidden, and the ruined city of Vardana inside the gods' ring of stones. About 30,000 live there, half in Ida on the south coast: the risen, the warped and the kept, ruled by the Daraya, whoever has done the greatest holding against what comes out of the ground. The Rot-Tyrant's legions come up out of the Dakhma, the bone-halls under Vardana. Origin of the Fleshwarp and Skeleton ancestries. The Adventurers' Guild posts its one perpetual standing warning.
