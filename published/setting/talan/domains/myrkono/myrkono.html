The Chains of the Bound

Myrkono is the cosmological heart of the post-binding world. All seven Wardstones (the relics that anchor the chains holding the Corrupted God beneath the earth) rest inside this domain, distributed across four of its sub-regions: two in Shadow Steppes, two in Itzasoa, two in Ilun Tasun, and the seventh (the Divine Wardstone) in Myrria itself. The concentration is not accidental: the adventurer fellowship that bound the Corrupted God was from Myrria, and the binding was anchored under Araphel's watch.

The ongoing struggle to keep the Wardstones safe, and to hunt down the Nine Generals of Corruption who emerge from their sealed dungeons across Talan, is the present condition of the domain. Read more in The Binding →

The Steppe Watch · Centaurs & Surki

Beside the Fetchling of the dark heartland, two peoples hold Myrkono that the rest of Talan does not picture when it thinks of the Darkness domain. They share an enemy and divide a single watch between the grass and the deep.

The Centaur Clans
The riders of the Shadow Steppes
The Shadow Steppes' own: riders whose clans have ranged the open grass for as long as the steppe record reaches. Whatever a Centaur feels, she feels at full speed and finishes, and the steppe gives the feeling room to run. The clans are the wandering watch the Wardstone record credits with guarding the open steppe: they ride between the Lakeside and Steppes Wardstones, running down corruption-spawn before a nest can root, under Araphel, for whom darkness is the cover and the quiet.
CentaurSteppe ridersWardstone wardens
The Surki
Insectoid sapients of the deep places
Climbers from the upper Darklands. Myrkono is the one domain that takes them in: Araphel's darkness asks nothing of the compound-eyed and chitinous, and Myrria's second-chances doctrine extends them the welcome other cities refuse. They live throughout the domain's deep and shadowed places, the mountain underways, the under-vaults below Myrria, the old-forest hollows, and most militantly the Shadow Steppes, where the Vermin Queen's Hollow seeds corruption outward. Surki are insectoid but not corrupted; resisting the Queen's swarm is the thing they exist to do.
Deep-dwellersAnti-corruption

On the Shadow Steppes the two peoples are the two halves of one watch: the centaur clans ride the grass above, the Surki climb the deep below, and they fight the same enemy from opposite sides of the ground. They keep no shared culture and little shared language. What they share is the Hollow, and the certainty that the chains of the bound must hold. Read more in The Binding →
