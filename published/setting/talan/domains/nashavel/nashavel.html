At a Glance

Etymology
Basque nahas (chaos, disorder, to mix) → rearranged and drifted. The chaos in the name itself.
Position
Northeast Talan, east of Ehizahar and Midarra, north of Zuzental and Egulon, coastal on Hafra
Terrain
The Basogur's canopy across the whole north, the dense old-growth jungle with wild magic woven into the trees, where paths do not stay fixed. South of it, open river-grassland and low hills farmed edge to edge (the Vernua Dominion), walled from Zuzental by the Hegandi escarpment; the lake Ispilur against the forest's edge in the northwest with two cities on it; the lone mountain Einbui in the middle; mountains in the east at the forest's edge on the Hafra coast.
Character
Dense ancient forest, wild magic zones

Vesuna: domain of Chaos
Peoples
Four dominant ancestries are anchored here, four stances toward the domain's one weather: Gnome (the ones who spin the change), Vanara (the ones who ride it), Anadi (the ones who re-weave after it; web-weavers of the Basogur and of Nahaskel's web-walks), and Conrasu (the ones who know what is underneath).

Sub-Regions

Vernua Dominion →
Vernua, Vesuna's name worn down; Dominion, the Empire's word, kept by the Dark-Era houses
The open middle of the domain and the country across the water from Nahaskel: a bowl whose weather never repeats, so nothing grows the same twice and everything that grows is made into something (the anois, the dish of what came; potin, the spirit of whatever the year gave). Governed by the Maors, the hereditary houses of the chain, over a countryside of comhar fellowships, bound by the Share; the capital Dunloch faces the god's city across the lake, and the city casts on the Maors' charter every few years and the coin comes up against them every time. The Conrasu home.

Kaosadaemi Principality →
Basque kaosa (chaos) + Icelandic dæmi (district): "the chaos district"; Principality the Thousand's word
The east of the domain, canopy to coast: a principality granted under Renauld's unified Thousand, orphaned by the Dark Era, and held by House Thornhollow, which went Gnome by staying. Chaos tuned to order: under the ring at Ringhold, the Tuning takes a Nahaskel spark and gives it a form that can be made twice, entered in the Prince's Second Book; at Greenmouth the Roadwards take travelers into the Basogur on a schedule no one else can keep. Houses wear the hair-colours of their tenants and the palace is the one grey building. The Gnome home.

Basogur Jungle
Basque baso (forest/wood) → drifted to Basogur
Dense jungle sub-region shared with Ehizahar, and the continent's rail-blocker: Basogur bisects the east-central waist of Talan, the reason there is no through-rail between the northern and southern Magitrain networks. Rails warp and runes fail in the jungle's chaos-touched magic, the indigenous Vanara, Anadi, and beast-spirits dismantle construction as it goes up, and the Nashavel/Ehizahar political deadlock locks any right of way. North-south traffic routes around it: stillship across the Midarra, airship over the canopy, or the long overland bypass.
